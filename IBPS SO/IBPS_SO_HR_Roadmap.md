## 2. Exam pattern

**Prelims** — 100 questions / 125 marks / 80 minutes total, sectional timing, sectional cutoffs apply:

| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Professional Knowledge | 50 | 50 | 20 min |
| Reasoning Ability | 50 | 25 | 20 min |
| Quantitative Aptitude | — | 25 | 20 min |
| English Language | — | 25 | 20 min |

**Mains** — 150 objective questions + 2 descriptive questions / 225 marks / 155 minutes:

- **Professional Knowledge (HR): 60 objective questions, 60 marks, 45 minutes — the ONLY Mains section that counts toward merit/interview shortlist.** Reasoning, Quant, English at Mains are qualifying only.
- Descriptive paper (English — essay/letter): 30 minutes, no negative marking.
- Negative marking: −0.25 per wrong objective answer (Prelims and Mains); descriptive has none.

**Takeaway: Professional Knowledge decides the outcome.** It's 50/125 marks at Prelims and the sole merit-determining section at Mains — weight prep time accordingly (see roadmap allocation below).

## 3. Subject-wise syllabus

### Professional Knowledge (HR) — the gap this doc fills
- HRM: concepts, scope, functions
- Organisational Behaviour: personality, perception, motivation theories, leadership theories, group dynamics
- HR Planning, Job Analysis
- Recruitment & Selection
- Training & Development
- Performance Management & Appraisal
- Compensation & Benefits Management
- Industrial Relations
- Labour Laws (Part 1 & 2) / **Labour Codes** (4 codes: Wages, Industrial Relations, Social Security, OSH — highest-yield, most-neglected area)
- Trade Unions & Collective Bargaining
- HR terminology / current HR practices in banking

*Note: IBPS does not publish a fixed topic list for this paper — the above is compiled from consistent previous-year question trends. Treat PYQs as the real syllabus.*

### Quantitative Aptitude
Fully covered in `IBPS_SO_HR_Quantitative_Aptitude_Simple_English_Guide.pdf` — use its 36-step sequence and topic-priority tiers directly. Don't duplicate here.

### Reasoning Ability
Puzzles & seating arrangement (linear, circular, floor-based), syllogism, coding-decoding, inequalities, blood relations, direction sense, order & ranking, data sufficiency, input-output.

### English Language
Reading comprehension, cloze test, error spotting, sentence improvement, para-jumbles, fill in the blanks, vocabulary (esp. for the Mains descriptive paper — essay/letter writing on HR/banking topics).

## 4. Long-runway roadmap (target: 2027 cycle)

Phased, not calendar-dated — slot real dates in once CRP SPL-XVII is announced.

**Phase 1 — Foundation (now → ~month 4)**
- Professional Knowledge: one HRM + OB textbook cover to cover (e.g. Aswathappa), chapter by chapter, notes per chapter.
- Quant: start the existing guide's 36-step sequence, Mathematical Foundation first.
- Reasoning & English: topic-by-topic basics, one new topic every 2–3 days each.

**Phase 2 — Depth & current-affairs layer (~month 5–8)**
- Labour Codes/Laws deep dive — dedicated block, this is where most candidates are weakest.
- Banking & HR current affairs tracking (monthly compilations).
- Sectional practice sets, one per subject per week.

**Phase 3 — PYQs & sectional tests (~month 9–10)**
- Previous-year Professional Knowledge questions — full set, timed.
- Sectional mocks per subject.
- Mistake log review (same "record mistakes → revise" loop as the Quant guide).

**Phase 4 — Full mocks & revision (last ~6–8 weeks before actual Prelims, once 2027 dates confirmed)**
- Full-length Prelims mocks, sectional-timed.
- After clearing Prelims: switch to Mains-pattern drilling — Professional Knowledge objective sets + descriptive (essay/letter) practice under 30-min timing.

**Checkpoint: re-check `ibps.in` for the CRP SPL-XVII notification around mid-2027 and re-anchor Phase 4 against the real date.**
