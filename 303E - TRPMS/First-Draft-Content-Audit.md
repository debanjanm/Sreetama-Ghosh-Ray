# First-draft content and writing audit

**Reviewed:** 11 September 2026  
**Original:** Performance-Pay-Pay-Structure-Report.original.md  
**Rewritten report:** Performance-Pay-Pay-Structure-Report.md

## Overall finding

The draft needed a substantive rewrite. Its repetitive bullet structure, compressed jargon and confident explanations made it sound formulaic. Those characteristics cannot establish AI authorship. The more consequential problem was that plausible generalisations were presented as verified company practices.

The rewrite retains the assignment’s three organisations and three topics, but replaces the prose, removes unsupported salary figures, dates policy reporting and places source links beside the claims they support. The original has been preserved unchanged.

## Writing changes

The revised version uses connected explanations instead of repeated promotional bullets. It removes phrases such as “structural lever” and “cost-and-compliance-driven redesign,” explains why payment timing and risk matter, and separates facts from interpretation. It also distinguishes performance pay, pay structure and salary structure at the outset.

## Remaining limitation

No internal compensation letters, payslips, grade schedules or assignment rubric were supplied. The report is a sourced public-information comparison. It does not claim to reproduce confidential company salary structures. If the assignment specifically requires authentic salary documents or grade-wise amounts, that requirement remains dependent on obtaining suitable evidence.
