**Research date:** 9 October 2026 (India time)

## 30-second overview

Infosys Limited is an Indian-founded global technology services and consulting company. Established in 1981, it helps clients with digital transformation, including AI, cloud, data, and technology operations. Its company overview says it serves clients in **59 countries**. Its FY 2025–26 report records revenue of **₹1,78,650 crore**; its investor FAQ reports **328,062 employees as of 30 June 2026**. State the date whenever quoting the workforce figure. [1][2][3][4]

## Why HR matters at Infosys

Infosys is a large, geographically dispersed, skills-intensive employer. Its published people material discusses digital learning through **Lex**, employee experience, and inclusion. The FY26 employee experience page reports an average of **113.3 training hours per employee**; the digital skilling page describes Lex as an AI-enabled learning platform. These are company disclosures, not promises about the specific HRD team. [6][7]

**Potential HRD work themes (inferences, not confirmed responsibilities of this opening):**

- Hiring, onboarding, and maintaining accurate employee records at scale.
- Learning, skills mapping, and career development as client technology needs change.
- Employee relations, manager support, communication, and consistent policy application across locations.
- Fair and confidential use of employee information and people analytics.

The actual practice options in the registration sheet may be narrower or different. Read the official mail and job description before choosing preferences.
