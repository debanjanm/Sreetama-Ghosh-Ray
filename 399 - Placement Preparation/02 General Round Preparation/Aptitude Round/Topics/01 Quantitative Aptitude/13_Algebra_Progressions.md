# Algebra, Equations, and Progressions

## Rules and formulas
- For ax²+bx+c=0, sum of roots=−b/a; product=c/a.
- AP: nth term a+(n−1)d; sum n[2a+(n−1)d]/2.
- GP: nth term ar^(n−1); finite sum a(r^n−1)/(r−1) when r≠1.

## Recognise the pattern
Constant difference → AP; constant ratio → GP. “Sum/product of roots” → coefficients, no need to solve. Word problem → define variables and units.

## Solve
1. 2x²−7x+3=0: sum roots 7/2, product 3/2; factor (2x−1)(x−3), roots **1/2 and 3**.
2. AP 5,8,11,...: a=5,d=3; 10th term 5+9×3=**32**; first 10 sum 10(5+32)/2=**185**.
3. GP 2,6,18,...: 5th term 2×3⁴=**162**.

## Tips and traps
Check r=1 separately in GP sum. For quadratics, discriminant b²−4ac tells whether real roots exist. Verify candidate roots by substitution.

## Try it
For x²−5x+6=0, roots? **Answer:** 2 and 3.

## More worked patterns

### Pattern 1: Solve 3(x−2)=2x+7.

**Solution:** Expand: 3x−6=2x+7, hence **x=13**; substitute to check both sides equal 33.

### Pattern 2: An arithmetic sequence starts at 5 and increases by 3. Find its 20th term.

**Solution:** aₙ=a+(n−1)d, so a₂₀=5+19×3=**62**.

### Pattern 3: Find the sum of the first 8 terms of 2, 6, 18, ...

**Solution:** Geometric ratio=3. S₈=2(3⁸−1)/(3−1)=**6,560**.
