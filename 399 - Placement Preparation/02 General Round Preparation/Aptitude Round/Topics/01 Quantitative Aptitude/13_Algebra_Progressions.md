# Algebra, Equations, and Progressions

> **Difficulty:** Medium to Hard  ·  **Target time:** 75 s

## In simple words

**The idea:** Algebra is arithmetic with a mystery number written as a letter. Keep both sides of the equation balanced.

**Think of it like:** A balance scale: whatever you do to one side, do to the other.

**Simple steps:**
1. Give the unknown a letter.
2. Write what the sentence says as an equation.
3. Simplify step by step, doing the same on both sides.
4. Check by putting your answer back in. An AP adds the same jump each time; a GP multiplies by the same number each time.

**Watch out:** When you multiply or divide an inequality by a negative number, flip the sign.

## Rules and formulas
- For ax²+bx+c=0, sum of roots=−b/a; product=c/a.
- AP: nth term a+(n−1)d; sum n[2a+(n−1)d]/2.
- GP: nth term ar^(n−1); finite sum a(r^n−1)/(r−1) when r≠1.

## Recognise the pattern
Constant difference → AP; constant ratio → GP. “Sum/product of roots” → coefficients, no need to solve. Word problem → define variables and units.

## Solve
1. 2x²−7x+3=0: sum roots 7/2, product 3/2; factor (2x−1)(x−3), roots **1/2 and 3**.
2. AP 5,8,11,...: a=5,d=3; 10th term 5+9×3=**32**; first 10 sum 10(5+32)/2=**185**.
3. GP 2,6,18,...: 5th term 2×3⁴=**162**.

## Tips and traps
Check r=1 separately in GP sum. For quadratics, discriminant b²−4ac tells whether real roots exist. Verify candidate roots by substitution.

## Try it
For x²−5x+6=0, roots? **Answer:** 2 and 3.

## More worked patterns

### Pattern 1: Solve 3(x−2)=2x+7.

**Solution:** Expand: 3x−6=2x+7, hence **x=13**; substitute to check both sides equal 33.

### Pattern 2: An arithmetic sequence starts at 5 and increases by 3. Find its 20th term.

**Solution:** aₙ=a+(n−1)d, so a₂₀=5+19×3=**62**.

### Pattern 3: Find the sum of the first 8 terms of 2, 6, 18, ...

**Solution:** Geometric ratio=3. S₈=2(3⁸−1)/(3−1)=**6,560**.

## Deeper patterns (medium to hard)

### Extra formulas and shortcuts
- Identities: (a±b)² = a² ± 2ab + b²; a²−b² = (a−b)(a+b); a³+b³ = (a+b)(a²−ab+b²).
- From sum and product: a²+b² = (a+b)² − 2ab. Also x² + 1/x² = (x+1/x)² − 2.
- Indices: aᵐ·aⁿ = aᵐ⁺ⁿ; (aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐⁿ; a⁰=1; a⁻ⁿ = 1/aⁿ.
- Simultaneous equations: eliminate one variable (multiply to match coefficients) then substitute.
- Inequalities: multiplying or dividing by a negative number flips the sign.
- Sum of first n odd numbers = n²; sum of first n squares = n(n+1)(2n+1)/6.
- Test options when the equation is awkward: plugging in four options is often faster than solving.

### Medium patterns

**M1. Solve 2x+3y=12 and 3x−y=7.**

**Solution:** y=3x−7 ⇒ 2x+9x−21 = 12 ⇒ x=3, y=2. **(3, 2)**.

**M2. x+y=10 and xy=21. Find x²+y².**

**Solution:** 100 − 42 = **58**.

**M3. Solve −2x + 3 ≤ 11.**

**Solution:** −2x ≤ 8; dividing by −2 flips the sign, so **x ≥ −4**.

### Hard patterns

**H1. x + 1/x = 5. Find x² + 1/x².**

**Solution:** 25 − 2 = **23**.

**H2. Sum of 1+3+5+…+49, and of 1²+2²+…+10²?**

**Solution:** 25 odd numbers, so 25² = **625**; squares: 10×11×21/6 = **385**.
