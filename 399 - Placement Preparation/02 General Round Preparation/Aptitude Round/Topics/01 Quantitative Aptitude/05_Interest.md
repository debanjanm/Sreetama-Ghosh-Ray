# Simple and Compound Interest

> **Difficulty:** Easy to Medium  ·  **Target time:** 60 s

## In simple words

**The idea:** Interest is rent paid for using money. Simple interest charges rent only on the original amount. Compound interest also charges rent on the rent already added.

**Think of it like:** Simple: a flat rent every year. Compound: a rent that grows because last year's unpaid rent is also charged.

**Simple steps:**
1. Note the principal P, the yearly rate R and the time T.
2. Simple interest = P × R × T ÷ 100.
3. Compound: multiply by (1 + R/100) once for every year (or period).
4. Compound interest = final amount − P.

**Watch out:** Check whether the question asks for the amount (P + interest) or just the interest, and whether compounding is yearly or half-yearly.

## Rules and formulas
- SI = PRT/100 for annual rate R and time T in years.
- Compound amount A=P(1+R/100)^T for annual compounding; CI=A−P.
- For exactly two annual periods at unchanged rate, CI−SI=P(R/100)².
- Half-yearly compounding: rate per period R/2, number of periods 2T.

## Recognise the pattern
“Interest on original principal each year” → SI. “Interest on accumulated amount” → CI. Check compounding frequency.

## Solve
1. ₹10,000 at 10% SI for two years: 10,000×10×2/100=**₹2,000**.
2. Same principal/rate at annual CI: amount 10,000×1.1²=₹12,100; interest **₹2,100**.
3. At 12% nominal annual rate, half-yearly for one year: 10,000×1.06²=₹11,236, so CI **₹1,236**.

## Tips and traps
Read whether the question asks for amount or interest. The two-year SI/CI difference shortcut does not apply unchanged to three years or irregular rates.

## Try it
₹5,000 at 8% annual CI for two years: interest? **Answer:** 5,000(1.08²−1)=₹832.

## More worked patterns

### Pattern 1: Find simple interest on ₹8,000 at 7.5% a year for 2 years.

**Solution:** SI=PRT/100=8000×7.5×2/100=**₹1,200**; amount=₹9,200.

### Pattern 2: Find compound interest on ₹10,000 for 2 years at 10% annually.

**Solution:** Amount=10000(1.1)²=₹12,100; CI=**₹2,100**. The second year earns interest on the first year’s interest.

### Pattern 3: At simple interest, a sum doubles in 8 years. When will it triple at the same rate?

**Solution:** Doubling earns one principal in 8 years. Tripling earns two principals, requiring **16 years**.

## Deeper patterns (medium to hard)

### Extra formulas and shortcuts
- Depreciation: value = P(1 − r/100)ⁿ.
- CI − SI for 3 years = P(r/100)²(3 + r/100).
- At SI, a sum becomes k times itself in 100(k−1)/r years; doubling in n years means rate 100/n %.
- Effective annual rate for half-yearly compounding = (1 + r/200)² − 1.
- Equal annual installments x for 2 years at r (as a decimal): loan = x/(1+r) + x/(1+r)².

### Medium patterns

**M1. At SI a sum is ₹6,000 after 2 years and ₹6,500 after 3 years. Principal and rate?**

**Solution:** Interest per year = ₹500. Principal = 6,000 − 1,000 = **₹5,000**; rate = 500/5,000 = **10%**.

**M2. CI minus SI on ₹8,000 for 2 years is ₹20. Rate?**

**Solution:** 8,000(r/100)² = 20 ⇒ (r/100)² = 0.0025 ⇒ **5%**.

### Hard patterns

**H1. A sum becomes ₹1,331 in 3 years at 10% CI, annual. Principal and CI?**

**Solution:** P = 1,331/1.331 = **₹1,000**; CI = **₹331**.

**H2. A loan of ₹2,100 is cleared in two equal annual installments at 10% CI. Installment?**

**Solution:** x(1/1.1 + 1/1.21) = x×2.1/1.21 = 2,100 ⇒ x = **₹1,210**. Check: 2,100×1.1=2,310−1,210=1,100; ×1.1=1,210.
