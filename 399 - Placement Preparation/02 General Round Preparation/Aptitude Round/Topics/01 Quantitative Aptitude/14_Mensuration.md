# Mensuration and Units

## Rules and formulas
- Rectangle area=lw; perimeter=2(l+w). Circle area=πr²; circumference=2πr.
- Cuboid volume=lwh. Cylinder volume=πr²h; curved area=2πrh; total area=2πr(h+r).
- Cone volume=πr²h/3. Sphere volume=4πr³/3.

## Recognise the pattern
“Capacity/melt and recast” → volume conservation. “Paint outside” → surface area. “Fence around” → perimeter. Check whether top/bottom are open.

## Solve
1. Cylinder radius 3 cm, height 10 cm: volume π×9×10=**90π cm³**.
2. A 4 m by 3 m floor has area 12 m² = **120,000 cm²**, because 1 m²=10,000 cm².
3. 2 m×3 m rectangle fencing requires 2(2+3)=**10 m**.

## Tips and traps
Area units are squared, volume cubed. Diameter is twice radius. Equate volumes for recasting only when material loss is ignored or explicitly ruled out.

## Try it
Circle diameter 14 cm: area? **Answer:** 49π cm² (154 cm² if π=22/7).

## More worked patterns

### Pattern 1: Find the area of a circular garden of radius 7 m. Use π=22/7.

**Solution:** Area=πr²=(22/7)×49=**154 m²**. Perimeter would use 2πr.

### Pattern 2: A cylinder has radius 3 cm and height 10 cm. Find volume.

**Solution:** Volume=πr²h=π×9×10=**90π cm³**.

### Pattern 3: A rectangular room is 8 m by 6 m. A 1 m wide path runs inside along all four walls. Find the path area.

**Solution:** Outer area=48 m²; inner rectangle is 6×4=24 m². Path area=**24 m²**.
