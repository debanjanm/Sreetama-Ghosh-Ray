# Mensuration and Units

> **Difficulty:** Medium to Hard  ·  **Target time:** 75 s

## In simple words

**The idea:** Perimeter is the length of the boundary, area is the flat space inside, and volume is the space inside a solid.

**Think of it like:** Perimeter is the fence around a plot, area is the carpet for the floor, volume is the water a tank holds.

**Simple steps:**
1. Name the shape.
2. Write its formula.
3. Make all units the same before putting numbers in.
4. Area answers are in square units; volume answers are in cube units.

**Watch out:** The radius is half the diameter. Check which one the question gives you.

## Rules and formulas
- Rectangle area=lw; perimeter=2(l+w). Circle area=πr²; circumference=2πr.
- Cuboid volume=lwh. Cylinder volume=πr²h; curved area=2πrh; total area=2πr(h+r).
- Cone volume=πr²h/3. Sphere volume=4πr³/3.

## Recognise the pattern
“Capacity/melt and recast” → volume conservation. “Paint outside” → surface area. “Fence around” → perimeter. Check whether top/bottom are open.

## Solve
1. Cylinder radius 3 cm, height 10 cm: volume π×9×10=**90π cm³**.
2. A 4 m by 3 m floor has area 12 m² = **120,000 cm²**, because 1 m²=10,000 cm².
3. 2 m×3 m rectangle fencing requires 2(2+3)=**10 m**.

## Tips and traps
Area units are squared, volume cubed. Diameter is twice radius. Equate volumes for recasting only when material loss is ignored or explicitly ruled out.

## Try it
Circle diameter 14 cm: area? **Answer:** 49π cm² (154 cm² if π=22/7).

## More worked patterns

### Pattern 1: Find the area of a circular garden of radius 7 m. Use π=22/7.

**Solution:** Area=πr²=(22/7)×49=**154 m²**. Perimeter would use 2πr.

### Pattern 2: A cylinder has radius 3 cm and height 10 cm. Find volume.

**Solution:** Volume=πr²h=π×9×10=**90π cm³**.

### Pattern 3: A rectangular room is 8 m by 6 m. A 1 m wide path runs inside along all four walls. Find the path area.

**Solution:** Outer area=48 m²; inner rectangle is 6×4=24 m². Path area=**24 m²**.

## Deeper patterns (medium to hard)

### Extra formulas and shortcuts
- Triangle ½bh; Heron √(s(s−a)(s−b)(s−c)) with s the semi-perimeter; equilateral (√3/4)a²; common right triples 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17.
- Parallelogram bh; trapezium ½(a+b)h; rhombus ½d₁d₂.
- Cube: V=a³, TSA 6a², diagonal a√3. Cuboid: TSA 2(lb+bh+hl), diagonal √(l²+b²+h²).
- Sphere area 4πr²; hemisphere curved 2πr², total 3πr². Cone slant l=√(r²+h²), CSA πrl, TSA πr(r+l).
- Polygon interior angle sum (n−2)×180°. Path or frame area = outer area − inner area.
- Flow problems: volume = cross-section × speed × time; 1 m³ = 1,000 L.

### Medium patterns

**M1. Area of a triangle with sides 13, 14, 15?**

**Solution:** s=21; √(21×8×7×6) = √7056 = **84**.

**M2. Total surface area of a 10×6×4 m cuboid?**

**Solution:** 2(60+24+40) = **248 m²**.

### Hard patterns

**H1. A sphere of radius 6 cm is melted into spheres of radius 2 cm. How many?**

**Solution:** (6/2)³ = **27**.

**H2. A cone has slant height 13 cm and base radius 5 cm. Find volume and curved area.**

**Solution:** Height 12; volume ⅓π×25×12 = **100π cm³**; CSA π×5×13 = **65π cm²**.

**H3. Water flows at 2 m/s through a pipe of cross-section 0.01 m². Litres in 1 minute?**

**Solution:** 0.01×2×60 = 1.2 m³ = **1,200 L**.
