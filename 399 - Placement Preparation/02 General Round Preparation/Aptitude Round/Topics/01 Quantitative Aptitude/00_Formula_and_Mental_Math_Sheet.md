## Mental math shortcuts
- ×5 = ×10÷2; ×25 = ×100÷4; ×9 = ×10 − itself; ×11 = add neighbouring digits.
- (n5)² = n(n+1) then 25, so 75² = 5,625.
- a² − b² = (a−b)(a+b), so 52² − 48² = 400.
- x% of y = y% of x.
- To check a division quickly, test divisibility: 3 and 9 by digit sum, 4 by last two digits, 11 by alternating digit sums.

## Test-day reminders
- Read what is asked: amount vs interest, profit vs profit percent, cost vs selling price.
- Convert units first; keep area units squared and volume units cubed.
- If options differ widely, approximate; if close, compute exactly.
- Skip any question that stalls you past its target time and come back later.
