# Speed, Distance, Trains, and Boats

> **Difficulty:** Medium  ·  **Target time:** 70 s

## In simple words

**The idea:** Speed tells you how much distance you cover per hour. Everything comes from: distance = speed × time.

**Think of it like:** Driving to a city: the distance is fixed, so a faster speed means less time.

**Simple steps:**
1. Make units match (km/h to m/s: multiply by 5/18).
2. Train crossing a platform: distance = train length + platform length.
3. Towards each other, add speeds; in the same direction, subtract.
4. Round trips: average speed = total distance ÷ total time.

**Watch out:** Going at 30 and returning at 60 does not give an average of 45. It is 40, because you spend longer at the slow speed.

## Rules and formulas
- Distance=speed×time; 1 km/h=5/18 m/s.
- For equal distances at speeds a,b: average speed=2ab/(a+b).
- Relative speed: opposite directions add; same direction subtract.
- Boat still-water speed b and stream speed s: downstream b+s, upstream b−s.

## Recognise the pattern
“Crosses a platform” → train length + platform length. “Round trip equal distances” → total distance/total time. “With/against current” → downstream/upstream.

## Solve
1. 120 m train crosses 180 m platform at 15 m/s: (120+180)/15=**20 s**.
2. 60 km out at 30 km/h, back at 60 km/h: 120/(2+1)=**40 km/h**.
3. Downstream 12 km/h and upstream 8 km/h: still-water speed (12+8)/2=**10 km/h**; stream **2 km/h**.

## Tips and traps
Convert units before substituting. When a train crosses a person, distance is the train length; when it crosses a platform, add both lengths.

## Try it
Two runners 18 km apart approach at 4 and 5 km/h. Meet in? **Answer:** 18/9=2 h.

## More worked patterns

### Pattern 1: A 120 m train passes a pole in 6 seconds. Find speed in km/h.

**Solution:** Speed=120/6=20 m/s; multiply by 3.6 to get **72 km/h**.

### Pattern 2: A boat travels 24 km downstream in 2 hours and upstream in 3 hours. Find still-water and stream speeds.

**Solution:** Downstream=12 km/h, upstream=8 km/h. Boat=(12+8)/2=**10 km/h**; stream=(12−8)/2=**2 km/h**.

### Pattern 3: Travel 60 km at 30 km/h and return at 60 km/h. Find average speed.

**Solution:** Total distance=120 km, time=2+1=3 h. Average=**40 km/h**, not 45.

## Deeper patterns (medium to hard)

### Extra formulas and shortcuts
- Two trains crossing each other: time = (L₁+L₂)/(relative speed); opposite directions add speeds, same direction subtract.
- For the same distance, speeds in ratio a:b take times in ratio b:a.
- Catch-up: head start d, speeds u > v → time = d/(u−v).
- Circular track of length L: meet every L/(u+v) in opposite directions, every L/(u−v) in the same direction.
- Late/early: distance = s₁s₂/(s₁−s₂) × (time difference).
- Boat round trip of d km each way: d/(b+s) + d/(b−s).
- Average speed over three equal parts = 3/(1/a+1/b+1/c).

### Medium patterns

**M1. Trains of 150 m and 100 m move in opposite directions at 60 and 40 km/h. Time to cross?**

**Solution:** Relative speed 100 km/h = 250/9 m/s; time = 250 ÷ (250/9) = **9 s**.

**M2. A walks at 5 km/h. B starts 2 h later from the same point at 7.5 km/h. When does B catch A?**

**Solution:** Head start 10 km; relative speed 2.5; **4 h** after B starts, at **30 km** from the start.

### Hard patterns

**H1. A man walking at 4 km/h is 15 minutes late; at 5 km/h he is 15 minutes early. Distance?**

**Solution:** d/4 − d/5 = 30/60 = 1/2 ⇒ d/20 = 1/2 ⇒ **10 km**.

**H2. A boat does 15 km/h in still water; the stream is 5 km/h. Total time for 30 km downstream and back?**

**Solution:** 30/20 + 30/10 = 1.5 + 3 = **4.5 h**.
