# Speed, Distance, Trains, and Boats

## Rules and formulas
- Distance=speed×time; 1 km/h=5/18 m/s.
- For equal distances at speeds a,b: average speed=2ab/(a+b).
- Relative speed: opposite directions add; same direction subtract.
- Boat still-water speed b and stream speed s: downstream b+s, upstream b−s.

## Recognise the pattern
“Crosses a platform” → train length + platform length. “Round trip equal distances” → total distance/total time. “With/against current” → downstream/upstream.

## Solve
1. 120 m train crosses 180 m platform at 15 m/s: (120+180)/15=**20 s**.
2. 60 km out at 30 km/h, back at 60 km/h: 120/(2+1)=**40 km/h**.
3. Downstream 12 km/h and upstream 8 km/h: still-water speed (12+8)/2=**10 km/h**; stream **2 km/h**.

## Tips and traps
Convert units before substituting. When a train crosses a person, distance is the train length; when it crosses a platform, add both lengths.

## Try it
Two runners 18 km apart approach at 4 and 5 km/h. Meet in? **Answer:** 18/9=2 h.
