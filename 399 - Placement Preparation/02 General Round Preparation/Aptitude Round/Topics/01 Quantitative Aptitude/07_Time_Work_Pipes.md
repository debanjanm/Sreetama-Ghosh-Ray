# Time and Work; Pipes

> **Difficulty:** Medium  ·  **Target time:** 70 s

## Rules and formulas
- If A takes a days, rate is 1/a task/day.
- Combined rate is the sum of rates; subtract an outlet/leak rate.
- Time = work remaining / net rate.

## Recognise the pattern
“Together,” “one leaves,” “pipe fills/empties,” “twice as efficient” → rates, not times. Efficiency and completion time are inverse.

## Solve
1. A takes 6 days, B 12: net 1/6+1/12=1/4; **4 days**.
2. Together for two days complete 1/2. A alone completes remaining 1/2 in (1/2)/(1/6)=**3 days**.
3. Inlet fills in 4 h, outlet empties in 12 h: rate 1/4−1/12=1/6; **6 h**.

## Tips and traps
Take LCM of given days as total units to avoid fractions. If net rate is zero or negative, the tank will not fill from empty. Clearly distinguish “A leaves after 2 days” from “B leaves.”

## Try it
A takes 10 days, B 15. Together? **Answer:** 1/(1/10+1/15)=6 days.

## More worked patterns

### Pattern 1: A completes a job in 12 days and B in 18 days. How long together?

**Solution:** Daily rates are 1/12 and 1/18; sum=5/36. Time=36/5=**7.2 days**.

### Pattern 2: A pipe fills a tank in 6 hours; a leak empties it in 9 hours. How long with both open?

**Solution:** Net fill rate=1/6−1/9=1/18 tank/hour, so **18 hours**.

### Pattern 3: A and B together finish in 10 days; A alone takes 15. Find B’s time.

**Solution:** B’s rate=1/10−1/15=1/30, so **30 days**.

## Deeper patterns (medium to hard)

### Extra formulas and shortcuts
- Man-days: M₁D₁H₁ / W₁ = M₂D₂H₂ / W₂.
- If A is x times as efficient as B, A takes 1/x of B's time.
- Alternate-day work: compute the 2-day cycle output, count full cycles, then finish the remainder day by day.
- Wages are divided in proportion to work done (rate × days worked).
- Pipes that open or close at different times: treat each interval separately at its net rate.

### Medium patterns

**M1. 8 men finish a job in 12 days at 6 h/day. Days for 6 men working 8 h/day?**

**Solution:** 8×12×6 = 6×D×8 ⇒ 576 = 48D ⇒ **12 days**.

**M2. A and B take 12 and 15 days. They work together for 5 days, then A leaves. How long will B take to finish?**

**Solution:** 5×(1/12+1/15) = 3/4 done; 1/4 left; B: 15×1/4 = **3¾ days**.

### Hard patterns

**H1. A (10 days) and B (15 days) work alternately, A first. Total time?**

**Solution:** Two-day output = 1/10+1/15 = 1/6. After 10 days (5 cycles) 5/6 is done, 1/6 left. Day 11, A does 1/10, leaving 1/15. Day 12, B does 1/15 and finishes. Total **12 days**.

**H2. A, B, C take 10, 15 and 30 days and are paid ₹9,000 for the job done together. Shares?**

**Solution:** Rates 1/10:1/15:1/30 = 3:2:1, equal days, so **₹4,500, ₹3,000, ₹1,500**.
