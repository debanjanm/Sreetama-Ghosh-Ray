# Mixtures and Alligation

## Rules and formulas
- Weighted concentration = (q₁c₁+q₂c₂)/(q₁+q₂).
- For low L, target M, high H with L<M<H: low quantity : high quantity = (H−M):(M−L).
- Replace x of a well-mixed volume V with pure solvent n times: remaining original fraction (1−x/V)^n.

## Recognise the pattern
“Mix two strengths/prices to obtain a target” → weighted average or alligation. “Remove and refill” → concentration falls by the fraction retained each round.

## Solve
1. Mix 20% and 50% to obtain 30%: low:high=(50−30):(30−20)=**2:1**.
2. Mix 2 L at 20% and 1 L at 50%: active amount .4+.5=.9 L out of 3 L → **30%**.
3. Ten litres of 40% solution: remove 2 L and refill water once. New concentration .4×(1−2/10)=**32%**.

## Tips and traps
The target must lie between ingredient concentrations. The replacement formula assumes full mixing and constant total volume. For different replacement amounts, multiply each remaining fraction separately.

## Try it
Mix 10% and 40% solutions for 25%. Ratio low:high? **Answer:** (40−25):(25−10)=1:1.
