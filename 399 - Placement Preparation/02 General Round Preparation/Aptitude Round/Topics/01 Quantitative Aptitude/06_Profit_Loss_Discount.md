# Profit, Loss, Markup, and Discount

## Rules and formulas
- Profit%=(SP−CP)/CP×100; loss%=(CP−SP)/CP×100.
- Markup is on cost; discount is on marked price.
- SP=MP(1−d/100). Two discounts a and b produce 1−(1−a/100)(1−b/100).

## Recognise the pattern
Mark-up then discount → use two multipliers. “Profit percentage” → divide by **cost**, even when marked price appears.

## Solve
1. CP ₹400; markup 25%, discount 10%. MP ₹500, SP ₹450; profit ₹50/₹400=**12.5%**.
2. Discounts 20% then 10%: final fraction .8×.9=.72; effective discount **28%**.
3. Item sold for ₹540 at 10% loss. CP=540/.9=**₹600**.

## Tips and traps
Assume CP=100 if only percentages matter. A 20% profit and 20% loss on equal cost items cancel; on equal selling prices they do not.

## Try it
CP ₹800, SP ₹920. Profit percent? **Answer:** 120/800=15%.
