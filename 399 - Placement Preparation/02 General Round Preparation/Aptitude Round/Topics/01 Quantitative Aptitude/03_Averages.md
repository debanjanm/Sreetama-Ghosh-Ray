# Averages

> **Difficulty:** Easy  ·  **Target time:** 40 s

## In simple words

**The idea:** The average is the amount each person would get if everything were shared equally.

**Think of it like:** Splitting a restaurant bill equally among friends.

**Simple steps:**
1. Total = average × number of items.
2. Change the total: add a new value, remove one, or swap one.
3. Divide the new total by the new count.

**Watch out:** Never just average two averages unless the two groups are the same size.

## Rules and formulas
- Average = total/count; total = average×count.
- Combined average = (n₁a₁+n₂a₂)/(n₁+n₂).
- Replacing one value changes average by (new−old)/count.

## Recognise the pattern
“One person joins/leaves,” “two groups combined,” or “one score was recorded incorrectly” → work with totals first.

## Solve
1. Ten people average 60, twenty average 75. Total =600+1500=2100, count=30; combined average **70**.
2. Six scores average 40. One recorded as 30 should be 48. Correct total =240−30+48=258; correct average **43**.
3. Five consecutive integers average 18. The middle integer is 18; sequence **16,17,18,19,20**.

## Tips and traps
The combined average lies between group averages. A plain mean of two averages is valid only if group sizes match. For “average increases by x after one joins,” new value = old average + (new count)×x.

## Try it
Eight values average 12; a ninth value of 21 joins. New average? **Answer:** (96+21)/9=13.

## More worked patterns

### Pattern 1: The average of 8 scores is 24. A score of 40 is replaced by 16. Find the new average.

**Solution:** Original sum=8×24=192. New sum=192−40+16=168; average=168/8=**21**.

### Pattern 2: A group of 12 has average age 20; a group of 8 has average age 25. Find their combined average.

**Solution:** Weighted sum=(12×20)+(8×25)=440 over 20 people; average=**22**. Do not simply average 20 and 25.

### Pattern 3: Five consecutive odd numbers average 37. Find the largest.

**Solution:** For a symmetric set the middle number is 37: 33, 35, 37, 39, **41**.

## Deeper patterns (medium to hard)

### Extra formulas and shortcuts
- Average of n consecutive numbers = (first + last)/2. Average of first n naturals = (n+1)/2; first n evens = n+1; first n odds = n.
- If a new member joins and the average rises by x, new member = old average + (new count)×x.
- Equal distances: average speed is the harmonic mean; equal times: it is the arithmetic mean.
- For three or more groups: Σ(nᵢaᵢ)/Σnᵢ.
- A group's total age after t years rises by t × (number of members); remember members who joined or left in between.

### Medium patterns

**M1. A class of 30 averages 15 years. Including the teacher raises the average by 1. Teacher's age?**

**Solution:** New total = 31×16 = 496; old total = 450; teacher = **46**.

**M2. Average of 5 numbers is 26; average of the first three is 22. Average of the last two?**

**Solution:** Total 130; first three 66; last two 64 so average **32**.

### Hard patterns

**H1. A batter's average after 20 innings is 42. What must be scored in the 21st to make it 44?**

**Solution:** 42 + 21×2 = **84**. Check: (840+84)/21 = 44.

**H2. The average age of a family of 5 is 24; the youngest is 4. What was the average age of the family just before the youngest was born?**

**Solution:** Total now 120; the other four now sum to 116; four years ago 116−16 = 100; average **25**.
