# Ratio and Proportion

> **Difficulty:** Easy  ·  **Target time:** 45 s

## Rules and formulas
- If quantities are in a:b and total T, they are Ta/(a+b), Tb/(a+b).
- a:b=c:d implies ad=bc.
- Partnership share is proportional to capital×time, if the agreement says so.

## Recognise the pattern
“Divide in ratio,” “ratio changes after joining,” “same quantity in two ratios” → assign one part x, then translate every statement into an equation.

## Solve
1. Divide 84 in 3:4. Seven parts=84; one=12; shares **36 and 48**.
2. Women:men=3:2 in a group of 50. Women=30, men=20. If five men join, ratio becomes 30:25=**6:5**.
3. A invests ₹2,000 for 12 months, B ₹3,000 for 8 months. Capital×time =24,000:24,000; share profit **equally**.

## Tips and traps
Keep “parts” until the final step. Ratio 3:2 does not imply the total is five people. When two ratios share a term, scale that term to a common value before combining.

## Try it
Two groups are in 5:7 and total 96. Larger group? **Answer:** 56.

## More worked patterns

### Pattern 1: Divide ₹840 in the ratio 2:3:7.

**Solution:** There are 12 parts, each ₹70. Shares are **₹140, ₹210, ₹490**; their sum checks to ₹840.

### Pattern 2: A:B=3:4 and B:C=6:5. Find A:B:C.

**Solution:** Make B equal: multiply the first ratio by 3 and the second by 2. Thus **9:12:10**.

### Pattern 3: If 6 workers finish a task in 15 days, how many days will 10 equally productive workers need?

**Solution:** Total work=6×15=90 worker-days; 90/10=**9 days**. This is inverse proportion.

## Deeper patterns (medium to hard)

### Extra formulas and shortcuts
- Compound ratio of a:b and c:d is ac : bd.
- Mean proportional between a and b = √(ab); third proportional to a and b = b²/a.
- If a/b = c/d, then each ratio also equals (a+c)/(b+d).
- Direct variation: more of one, more of the other. Inverse: more men, fewer days (product constant).
- If incomes are in a:b and expenditures in c:d with equal savings, set 'ax−cy = bx−dy = savings' and solve for x, y.

### Medium patterns

**M1. Incomes of A and B are in 5:4 and expenditures in 3:2. Each saves ₹1,600. Find incomes.**

**Solution:** Incomes 5x, 4x; expenditures 3y, 2y. 5x−3y=1600 and 4x−2y=1600 give x=800, y=800. Incomes **₹4,000 and ₹3,200**.

**M2. A invests ₹12,000 and B ₹9,000. After 4 months C joins with ₹15,000. Annual profit ₹93,000. C's share?**

**Solution:** A: 12,000×12=144,000; B: 9,000×12=108,000; C: 15,000×8=120,000. Ratio 12:9:10, 31 parts of ₹3,000. C = **₹30,000**.

### Hard patterns

**H1. Milk:water is 3:2 in one vessel and 4:1 in another. Equal quantities are mixed. Resulting ratio?**

**Solution:** Milk = 3/5+4/5 = 7/5; water = 2/5+1/5 = 3/5. Ratio **7 : 3**.

**H2. If x:y = 3:4, find (2x+3y) : (3x+2y).**

**Solution:** x=3k, y=4k: (6+12):(9+8) = **18 : 17**.
