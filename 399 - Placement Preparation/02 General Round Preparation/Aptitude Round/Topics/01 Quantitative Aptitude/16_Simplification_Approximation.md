# Simplification and Approximation

> **Difficulty:** Easy  ·  **Target time:** 30 s

## In simple words

**The idea:** A long sum has an agreed order of steps, so everyone gets the same answer. That order is BODMAS.

**Think of it like:** It is the grammar of maths: it tells you which part to read first.

**Simple steps:**
1. Brackets first.
2. Then powers and roots.
3. Then division and multiplication, left to right.
4. Then addition and subtraction, left to right.

**Watch out:** Do not add before you multiply. 2 + 3 × 4 is 14, not 20.

## Rules and formulas
- BODMAS: Brackets, Orders (powers and roots), then Division and Multiplication left to right, then Addition and Subtraction left to right.
- Fractions: bring to a common denominator before adding; to compare fractions, cross-multiply.
- Decimals: count the total decimal places, multiply as integers, then place the point.
- x% of y = y% of x, so choose whichever is easier (8% of 50 = 50% of 8 = 4).
- Roots to remember: √2≈1.414, √3≈1.732, √5≈2.236, √6≈2.449, √7≈2.646.

## Recognise the pattern
A long chain of numbers with no story → BODMAS. "Approximately" or "?" with answer options far apart → round each number to a friendly value first. "Exact value" → do not round.

## Solve
1. 18 + 6 ÷ 3 × 2 − 4: divide and multiply first, 6÷3=2, 2×2=4, so 18+4−4 = **18**.
2. 35% of 240 + 15% of 160 = 84 + 24 = **108**.
3. Approximate 49.8 × 6.1 ÷ 2.02: round to 50 × 6 ÷ 2 = **150** (exact is about 150.4).
4. 75²: take 7×8 = 56 and append 25, giving **5,625**.

## Speed tricks
- ×5 = ×10 ÷ 2; ×25 = ×100 ÷ 4; ×50 = ×100 ÷ 2; ×9 = ×10 − itself; ×11: add neighbouring digits.
- Squares ending in 5: (n5)² = n(n+1) followed by 25.
- Numbers near 100: 98×97 = (100−2)(100−3) → 100−5=95 and 2×3=06, so 9,506.
- Difference of squares: 52² − 48² = (52−48)(52+48) = 400.

## Tips and traps
Round only when the options are clearly apart; if two options differ by under 2%, compute exactly. Do not skip brackets and powers because you "see" the answer. Keep a running sign check when subtracting a bracket.

## Try it
1. 25 × 48. **Answer:** 1,200.
2. 15% of 80 + 80% of 15. **Answer:** 12 + 12 = 24.
3. Approximate 399.8 ÷ 19.9. **Answer:** about 20.
