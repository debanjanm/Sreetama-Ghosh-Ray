# Direction Sense

> **Difficulty:** Easy  ·  **Target time:** 50 s

## In simple words

**The idea:** Track your position on a map one move at a time.

**Think of it like:** Following a walking route on a map app.

**Simple steps:**
1. Draw a cross with north at the top.
2. Note which way you face at the start.
3. A turn changes your facing; a walk moves you. Mark each move.
4. The final answer is the straight line from the start to the end.

**Watch out:** The distance you walked is not the same as how far you are from the start.

## Rules
North +y, south −y, east +x, west −x. Straight-line displacement = √(x²+y²). Distance travelled = sum of path lengths.

## Recognise and solve
1. Go 4 km north, 3 east, 4 south → endpoint (3,0): **3 km east**; distance walked **11 km**.
2. Go 6 west, 8 north → endpoint (−6,8): straight-line distance √(36+64)=**10 km** northwest.
3. Face north, turn right, then left → face **north**.

## Tips and traps
Reorient after every turn. “Left of a person facing south” is not left on the page. A return distance is displacement, not total journey.

## Try it
Go 5 east, 12 north. Shortest distance home? **Answer:** 13 km.

## More worked patterns

### Pattern 1: Walk 3 km east and 4 km north. How far and in what direction are you from the start?

**Solution:** The displacement is a right triangle: √(3²+4²)=**5 km northeast**. Distance walked is 7 km.

### Pattern 2: Facing north, turn right, then right again, then left. Which direction are you facing?

**Solution:** North→east→south→**east**. Track each turn on the current direction.

### Pattern 3: Walk 5 m north, 2 m east, then 5 m south. Where are you relative to start?

**Solution:** North and south cancel; the final point is **2 m east**.

## Deeper patterns (medium to hard)

### Extra rules and tricks
- Right-angle triples: 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17 give straight-line distances without a calculator.
- Shadows: in the morning the sun is in the east, so shadows fall to the west. In the evening shadows fall to the east.
- Turning rule: a right turn moves clockwise (N→E→S→W); a left turn moves anticlockwise.
- Diagonals: north-east, south-east, south-west and north-west sit halfway between the main directions.

### Medium patterns

**M1. A walks 10 m north, turns right and walks 5 m, then turns right and walks 10 m. How far and in which direction from the start?**

**Solution:** North 10, east 5, south 10 gives the point (5, 0): **5 m east**.

**M2. Facing south, a person turns left, then right, then right. Which direction is she facing?**

**Solution:** Facing south, left is east; right of east is south; right of south is west. She faces **west**.

### Hard patterns

**H1. Ravi walks 8 km west, 6 km north, 8 km east and 2 km south. Where is he from the start?**

**Solution:** x = −8+8 = 0; y = 6−2 = 4. He is **4 km north** of the start.

**H2. A man walks 12 m north then 9 m east. Straight-line distance from the start?**

**Solution:** √(12²+9²) = √225 = **15 m**, towards the north-east.

**H3. At 8 AM, Ria stands facing her own shadow. Which way is she facing?**

**Solution:** In the morning the shadow falls west, so she faces **west**.
