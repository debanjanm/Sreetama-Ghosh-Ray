# Blood Relations

## In simple words

**The idea:** Turn the words into a family tree picture and the answer becomes easy to read.

**Think of it like:** Drawing an org chart of who reports to whom, but for a family.

**Simple steps:**
1. Draw one row per generation.
2. Join parent and child with vertical lines, spouses and siblings with horizontal lines.
3. Mark gender only if the question says so.
4. Start the phrase from the person being asked about and read it step by step.

**Watch out:** Do not guess a person's gender from their name.

## Representation
Draw generations in rows. Use parent-child vertical links and spouse/sibling horizontal links. Mark gender only when stated.

## Recognise and solve
1. “A is B's mother. B is C's brother.” Under usual full-sibling placement convention, A is C's **mother**. A strict logical question may need more detail about parentage.
2. “Pointing to a man, Mira says: he is my mother's only son.” If Mira is female, he is her **brother**. If Mira's gender is unstated, do not infer it from the name.
3. “P is Q's father; Q is R's mother.” P is R's **maternal grandfather**.

## Tips and traps
Resolve “his/her” to a named person before drawing the next edge. “Only daughter” can refer to the speaker if gender permits. In strict reasoning, do not infer a relationship the words do not guarantee.

## Try it
Neha is Arjun's sister; Arjun is Kiran's father. Neha to Kiran? **Answer:** paternal aunt, assuming ordinary sibling relation.

## More worked patterns

### Pattern 1: Anita says, “He is the son of my mother’s only daughter.” Who is he to Anita?

**Solution:** The mother’s only daughter is Anita. Her son is Anita’s **son**. Draw the intermediate link before answering.

### Pattern 2: Ravi’s father is Meera’s brother. How is Meera related to Ravi?

**Solution:** Meera is the sister of Ravi’s father, so she is his **paternal aunt**.

### Pattern 3: A is B’s mother. C is A’s father. How is C related to B?

**Solution:** C is the father of B’s mother, hence B’s **maternal grandfather**.
