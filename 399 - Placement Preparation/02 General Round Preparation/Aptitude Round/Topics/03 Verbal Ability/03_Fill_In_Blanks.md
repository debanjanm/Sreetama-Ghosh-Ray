# Fill in the Blanks and Cloze

## In simple words

**The idea:** The sentence gives clues about the missing word: contrast, cause, example or tone.

**Think of it like:** Solving a crossword by using the crossing letters as clues.

**Simple steps:**
1. Read the words before and after the blank.
2. Spot clue words like 'although', 'because' and 'therefore'.
3. Predict the kind of word (positive, negative, noun, verb).
4. Test each option in the full sentence.

**Watch out:** For two blanks, solve the easier blank first, then check both together.

## Method
Read before/after the blank, identify contrast, cause, condition, or example; predict polarity and part of speech. For two blanks, solve the more constrained one first and test the pair together.

## Recognise and solve
1. “Although the deadline was short, the team remained ___.” Contrast supports **calm/organised**, depending on options, rather than “overwhelmed” if the sentence implies success.
2. “The evidence was ___; therefore, the conclusion was tentative.” **Limited/inconclusive** fits the causal relationship.
3. “The process was designed to be both fair and ___.” A parallel adjective such as **transparent** fits better than a noun.

## Tips and traps
Check “although,” “however,” “therefore,” and “despite” first. Do not choose by vocabulary alone; both grammar and logic must work. In cloze passages, track a consistent topic across blanks.

## Try it
“The findings were preliminary; ___, the team planned a larger study.” **Answer:** therefore/accordingly (depending on options).

## More worked patterns

### Pattern 1: Fill in: “Despite the tight deadline, the team remained ___ and completed the work carefully.” careless / composed / indifferent

**Solution:** **Composed** fits calm, effective performance despite pressure. “Despite” signals a contrast with expected stress.

### Pattern 2: Fill in: “The report was ___ by interviews and survey data.” contradicted / supported / ignored

**Solution:** **Supported** fits evidence backing a report. Check that the chosen word makes the whole sentence coherent.

### Pattern 3: Fill in both: “The manager was ___ about the delay but ___ the team for its transparency.” concerned; praised / delighted; blamed

**Solution:** **Concerned; praised** creates a coherent distinction between concern about timing and appreciation of honesty.
