## How to read your score

| Marks out of 70 | What it suggests |
|---|---|
| Below 28 | Learn the topic files first, then retake. Focus on 'In simple words' sections |
| 28 to 41 | Concepts are building. Fix the error log topics and retake in a week |
| 42 to 55 | Good. Work on speed and the hard patterns |
| Above 55 | Strong. Keep practising timed sets and the weakest section |

Many companies set a cut-off for each section, so a weak section matters even if your total is high.

## Section A: Quantitative Aptitude

**Q1. D** (15%). (46,000 − 40,000)/40,000 = 15%. *Topic: Percentages.*

**Q2. C** (25%). 0.8 × x = 1, so x = 1.25: a 25% rise. *Topic: Percentages.*

**Q3. B** (22). Total 150; remove 40 → 110; 110/5 = 22. *Topic: Averages.*

**Q4. A** (₹600). 9 parts of ₹300 each; smallest = 2 × 300 = ₹600. *Topic: Ratio and Proportion.*

**Q5. A** (₹810). 4,500 × 6 × 3 / 100 = ₹810. *Topic: Interest.*

**Q6. D** (₹600). 480 × 1.25 = ₹600. *Topic: Profit, Loss and Discount.*

**Q7. C** (6 days). 1/15 + 1/10 = 1/6, so 6 days. *Topic: Time and Work.*

**Q8. D** (20 m/s). 72 × 5/18 = 20 m/s. *Topic: Speed and Distance.*

**Q9. C** (36). 12 = 2²·3 and 18 = 2·3², so LCM = 2²·3² = 36. *Topic: Number System.*

**Q10. D** (40). Son x, father 4x; 5x = 50, so x = 10 and the father is 40. *Topic: Ages.*

**Q11. B** (₹60). (3×50 + 2×75)/5 = 300/5 = ₹60. *Topic: Mixtures and Alligation.*

**Q12. D** (39). 144 ÷ 12 = 12; 12 × 3 = 36; 36 + 8 − 5 = 39. *Topic: Simplification.*

**Q13. B** (₹1,050). 5,000 × 1.1² = 6,050; CI = 6,050 − 5,000 = ₹1,050. *Topic: Interest.*

**Q14. A** (₹1,800). 2,500 × 0.8 × 0.9 = ₹1,800. *Topic: Profit, Loss and Discount.*

**Q15. D** (24 days). 12 × 18 = 216 man-days; 216/9 = 24 days. *Topic: Time and Work.*

**Q16. B** (20 s). 54 km/h = 15 m/s; distance = 300 m; time = 20 s. *Topic: Speed and Distance.*

**Q17. B** (3 hours). Downstream speed 12 km/h; 36/12 = 3 hours. *Topic: Speed and Distance.*

**Q18. D** (2 : 1). (60 − 40) : (40 − 30) = 20 : 10 = 2 : 1. *Topic: Mixtures and Alligation.*

**Q19. B** (48 km/h). 2×40×60/(40+60) = 48 km/h. *Topic: Speed and Distance.*

**Q20. A** (5/36). Sum 8: (2,6), (3,5), (4,4), (5,3), (6,2) = 5 of 36. *Topic: Probability.*

**Q21. B** (60). 6!/(3! × 2!) = 720/12 = 60. *Topic: Permutations and Combinations.*

**Q22. A** (14). (x + 1/x)² − 2 = 16 − 2 = 14. *Topic: Algebra.*

**Q23. C** (1,540 cm³). (22/7) × 49 × 10 = 1,540 cm³. *Topic: Mensuration.*

**Q24. C** (₹7,000). A: 6,000×12 = 72,000; B: 9,000×8 = 72,000. Equal, so A gets ₹7,000. *Topic: Partnership.*

**Q25. A** (No relation). x = 3, 4; y = 2, 4. (3,2) gives x > y but (3,4) gives x < y, so there is no relation. *Topic: Quadratic Equation Comparison.*

**Q26. A** (35%). Women = 30 + 40 = 70 of 200 = 35%. The plain average 37.5% is the trap. *Topic: Data Interpretation.*

**Q27. A** (70). (5k+10)/(7k+10) = 3/4 gives 20k + 40 = 21k + 30, so k = 10 and the elder is 70. *Topic: Ages.*

**Q28. B** (₹3,600). Rates 1/10 : 1/15 = 3 : 2; A gets 3/5 × 6,000 = ₹3,600. *Topic: Time and Work.*

**Q29. B** (79,200). 80,000 × 1.1 × 0.9 = 79,200. *Topic: Percentages.*

**Q30. A** (18). 180 = 2² × 3² × 5; (2+1)(2+1)(1+1) = 18. *Topic: Number System.*

## Section B: Logical Reasoning

**Q31. B** (79). Each term is double the previous plus 1: 39 × 2 + 1 = 79. *Topic: Number and Letter Series.*

**Q32. C** (20). Differences are 1, 2, 3, 4, so the next is 5: 15 + 5 = 20. *Topic: Number and Letter Series.*

**Q33. C** (X). Positions 10, 12, 15, 19 have gaps 2, 3, 4, so next gap 5: 24 = X. *Topic: Number and Letter Series.*

**Q34. B** (EFTL). Each letter moves forward by 1: D→E, E→F, S→T, K→L. *Topic: Coding and Decoding.*

**Q35. A** (ke). 'bright' is common to both sentences and 'pa' is common to both codes, so 'light' = ke. *Topic: Coding and Decoding.*

**Q36. A** (Paternal aunt). A is the sister of C's father, so A is C's paternal aunt. *Topic: Blood Relations.*

**Q37. C** (Brother). Meena's grandfather's only son is her father; his son is her brother. *Topic: Blood Relations.*

**Q38. B** (10 km). √(6² + 8²) = 10 km. *Topic: Direction Sense.*

**Q39. C** (West). East → right = south → right = west. *Topic: Direction Sense.*

**Q40. D** (Both I and II follow). All + All gives I. If all managers are mentors, then some mentors are managers, so II follows as well. *Topic: Syllogisms.*

**Q41. D** (Definitely follows). The files that are folders cannot be books, so some files are not books. *Topic: Syllogisms.*

**Q42. D** (10). Union = 55 + 40 − 15 = 80; neither = 90 − 80 = 10. *Topic: Venn Diagrams and Counting.*

**Q43. A** (45°). Hour hand = 4 × 30 + 15 = 135°; minute hand = 180°; difference 45°. *Topic: Clocks and Calendars.*

**Q44. C** (Wednesday). Neither 2001 nor 2002 is a leap year, so 2 odd days: Monday + 2 = Wednesday. *Topic: Clocks and Calendars.*

**Q45. D** (R). T=1, Q=2, R=3, S=4, P=5. The middle seat (3) is R. *Topic: Seating Arrangements.*

**Q46. C** (7). Y is 20 − 5 + 1 = 16th from the left; between = 16 − 8 − 1 = 7. *Topic: Ranking and Order.*

**Q47. B** (Pune). Bina = Goa. Chitra is not in Pune, so she is in Delhi. That leaves Asha in Pune. *Topic: Grid and Matching Puzzles.*

**Q48. C** (Statement 1 alone is sufficient). If x² is even, x must be even, so (1) is enough. Multiples of 3 can be odd or even, so (2) is not. *Topic: Data Sufficiency.*

**Q49. A** (Statement 1 alone is sufficient). (1) gives 120/4 = ₹30. (2) is about pens and does not help. *Topic: Data Sufficiency.*

**Q50. B** (Helmets can reduce head injuries). The advice works only if helmets can reduce head injuries. *Topic: Statements and Assumptions.*

## Section C: Verbal Ability

**Q51. B** (Wise). Prudent means careful and sensible, so 'wise' is closest. *Topic: Vocabulary and Usage.*

**Q52. D** (Abundant). Scarce means in short supply; the opposite is abundant. *Topic: Vocabulary and Usage.*

**Q53. C** (affect). 'Affect' is the verb meaning to influence. 'Effect' is usually a noun. 'Will' needs the base verb. *Topic: Vocabulary and Usage.*

**Q54. D** (The list of items is on the table.). The subject is 'list', which is singular, so the verb is 'is'. *Topic: Grammar and Error Spotting.*

**Q55. C** (for). 'For' goes with a duration; 'since' needs a starting point. *Topic: Grammar and Error Spotting.*

**Q56. D** (were). A hypothetical condition takes 'were'. *Topic: Grammar and Error Spotting.*

**Q57. D** (to). 'Senior to' is the correct fixed phrase. *Topic: Grammar and Error Spotting.*

**Q58. A** (confident). 'Although' signals contrast with 'risky', so a positive word like 'confident' fits. *Topic: Fill in the Blanks.*

**Q59. D** (clear). 'So … that everyone understood' needs a positive quality: clear. *Topic: Fill in the Blanks.*

**Q60. B** (forced). Rising costs pressure the company, so 'forced' fits. *Topic: Fill in the Blanks.*

**Q61. C** (To work late at night). The idiom means working late into the night. *Topic: Idioms and One-word Substitutions.*

**Q62. B** (Nepotism). Nepotism is favouring relatives or friends in appointments. *Topic: Idioms and One-word Substitutions.*

**Q63. C** (QRP). Q introduces 'an online portal'; R explains what it allowed; P gives the result. *Topic: Para Jumbles.*

**Q64. A** (BCDA). Orders rose (B), the team could not cope (C), delays began (D), therefore more staff (A). *Topic: Para Jumbles.*

**Q65. B** (Training may have helped, but the waiting-time change could also explain the fall). Two changes happened together, so the passage supports only a cautious conclusion. *Topic: Reading Comprehension.*

**Q66. B** (Complaints also fell at branches where clerks were trained but waiting time did not change). A case with training but no change in waiting time separates the two causes. *Topic: Reading Comprehension.*

**Q67. A** (A salary hike was announced in the same month). A salary hike gives another explanation for the rise. *Topic: Critical Reasoning.*

**Q68. D** (Customers in the evening would use a night counter). The proposal works only if customers would actually use the night counter. *Topic: Critical Reasoning.*

**Q69. B** (at). 'Good at' is the fixed phrase. *Topic: Grammar and Error Spotting.*

**Q70. D** (was). 'Information' is uncountable and takes a singular verb. *Topic: Grammar and Error Spotting.*

2. Label the cause: concept, formula, arithmetic slip, misread, time pressure, or guess.
3. Re-read the matching topic file, then redo the question after one day without looking at the answer.
