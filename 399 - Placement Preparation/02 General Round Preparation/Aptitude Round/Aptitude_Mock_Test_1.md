# Aptitude Mock Test 1

A full timed mock for general placement aptitude. It is practice material and does not claim to reproduce any employer's actual test. Check the real test's pattern, timing and marking rules when they are announced.

## Instructions

- **70 questions, 75 minutes.** Keep the three time limits below; do not borrow time from another section.
- Solve on paper. No calculator. Keep the [Formula and Mental Math Sheet](Topics/01%20Quantitative%20Aptitude/00_Formula_and_Mental_Math_Sheet.md) closed during the test.
- Marking (practice rule): **+1 for each correct answer, −0.25 for each wrong answer, 0 for a skipped question.** Change this if the real test differs.
- If a question takes more than about a minute and a half, mark it and move on. Come back if time remains.
- Write your answers on the grid at the end, then check them against the [Answer Key](Aptitude_Mock_Test_1_Answer_Key.md).

| Section | Questions | Time |
|---|---:|---|
| Quantitative Aptitude | 30 | 35 minutes |
| Logical Reasoning | 20 | 22 minutes |
| Verbal Ability | 20 | 18 minutes |
| **Total** | **70** | **75 minutes** |

## Section A: Quantitative Aptitude (30 questions, 35 minutes)

**Q1.** A town's population rises from 40,000 to 46,000. What is the percentage increase?

- A) 14%
- B) 16%
- C) 12%
- D) 15%

**Q2.** A price falls by 20%. By what percent must it rise to return to the original price?

- A) 20%
- B) 30%
- C) 25%
- D) 22.5%

- A) 21
- B) 22
- C) 20
- D) 23

## Section C: Verbal Ability (20 questions, 18 minutes)

**Q51.** Choose the synonym of 'prudent'.

- A) Lazy
- B) Wise
- C) Careless
- D) Angry

**Q52.** Choose the antonym of 'scarce'.

- A) Rare
- B) Limited
- C) Little
- D) Abundant

**Q53.** 'The new policy will ___ every employee.'

- A) effect
- B) affects
- C) affect
- D) effects

**Q54.** Choose the correct sentence.

- A) The lists of items is on the table.
- B) The list of items were on the table.
- C) The list of items are on the table.
- D) The list of items is on the table.

**Q55.** 'She has been teaching here ___ ten years.'

- A) since
- B) from
- C) for
- D) by

**Q56.** 'If I ___ you, I would take the offer.'

- A) am
- B) was
- C) is
- D) were

**Q57.** 'He is senior ___ me in the company.'

- A) than
- B) over
- C) from
- D) to

**Q58.** 'Although the plan looked risky, the manager remained ___.'

- A) confident
- B) confused
- C) worried
- D) silent

**Q59.** 'The trainer's explanation was so ___ that everyone understood it at once.'

- A) vague
- B) long
- C) boring
- D) clear

**Q60.** 'Rising costs ___ the company to cut its budget.'

- A) allowed
- B) forced
- C) helped
- D) invited

**Q61.** 'To burn the midnight oil' means:

- A) To start a fire
- B) To waste money
- C) To work late at night
- D) To sleep early

**Q62.** One word for 'favouring relatives when giving jobs':

- A) Meritocracy
- B) Nepotism
- C) Plagiarism
- D) Bureaucracy

**Q63.** Arrange: P. The portal reduced waiting time. Q. The bank launched an online portal. R. Customers could now apply without visiting a branch.

- A) PQR
- B) RPQ
- C) QRP
- D) QPR

**Q64.** Arrange: A. Therefore, the firm hired more staff. B. Orders increased sharply in March. C. The existing team could not cope. D. Delays began to appear.

- A) BCDA
- B) DBCA
- C) CBDA
- D) BDCA

**Q65.** Passage: 'A bank trained 50 clerks in customer service. Complaints fell by 18% in the next quarter. However, the bank also reduced waiting time at counters during the same quarter.' What is the best conclusion?

- A) Complaints increased after training
- B) Training may have helped, but the waiting-time change could also explain the fall
- C) Training alone caused the fall in complaints
- D) Waiting time had no effect on complaints

**Q66.** Using the same passage, which new fact would best show that training helped?

- A) The bank hired more clerks
- B) Complaints also fell at branches where clerks were trained but waiting time did not change
- C) Complaints fell at branches with no training
- D) Waiting time fell at every branch

**Q67.** 'Employee satisfaction rose after the cafeteria was renovated, so the renovation caused it.' Which fact most weakens this?

- A) A salary hike was announced in the same month
- B) The renovation cost ₹5 lakh
- C) The cafeteria is on the ground floor
- D) Some employees do not eat there

**Q68.** 'We should open a night counter because queues are long in the evening.' What is the assumption?

- A) Queues are long every day
- B) Night counters are cheap
- C) All customers prefer evenings
- D) Customers in the evening would use a night counter

**Q69.** 'She is good ___ analysing data.'

- A) with
- B) at
- C) in
- D) on

**Q70.** 'The information provided ___ useful.'

- A) are
- B) were
- C) have been
- D) was
