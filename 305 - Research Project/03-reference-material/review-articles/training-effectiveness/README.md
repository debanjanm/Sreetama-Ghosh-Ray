# Training Effectiveness Article Collection

This collection supports the dependent variable: **Training Effectiveness**. In Version 2, training effectiveness means the extent to which employees consider a recent hybrid-training programme useful, understandable, relevant to their work, and capable of improving the application of knowledge and skills.

## Source Use

| Source | Type | Use in Version 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Aziz (2015) | Workplace scale-development journal article | Principal item foundation for learning performance and individual work improvement. |
| Baldwin and Ford (1988) | Foundational training-transfer article | Explains why learning must be applied at work to be effective. |
| Holton (1996) | Training-evaluation theory article | Supports a broader view of effectiveness beyond immediate trainee reaction. |
| Blume et al. (2010) | Training-transfer meta-analysis | Supports the importance of work application and the limits of self-report. |
| Uddin et al. (2026) | Workplace blended-learning journal article | Connects blended learning, knowledge-related development, and employee performance. |
