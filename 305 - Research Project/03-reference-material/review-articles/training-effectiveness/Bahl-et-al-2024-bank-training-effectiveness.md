## Verified Study Details

- **Population:** 402 managerial and non-managerial respondents from public, private, and foreign-sector banks in India.
- **Model:** Kirkpatrick’s Reaction → Learning → Behaviour → Results framework.
- **Focus:** Evaluation of employee training effectiveness in banking. The Results level includes individual and bank-performance outcomes.
- **Analysis:** PLS-SEM; the reported adjusted R² for Results is .732 for managerial employees and .571 for non-managerial employees.

## Use in Version 3

Bahl, Kiran and Sharma is the **Indian banking training-effectiveness base paper**. It supports the importance of evaluating employee training in a banking setting and helps establish the banking-sector gap.

It is **not** used as evidence for Hybrid Learning, Trainer Competence, Learner Engagement, Union Vidya, or the three Version 3 relationship hypotheses. The current study does not reproduce the Kirkpatrick model or ask employees to assess Bank-wide performance.
