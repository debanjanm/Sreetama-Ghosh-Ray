# Union Vidya LMS Context — Official Source Record

## Purpose

This record documents the official Union Bank of India information used to describe the local learning environment in Version 3. It supports organisational context only. It is not a research paper, a measurement scale, or evidence that every employee has the same learning experience.

## Verified Organisational Facts

| Source | Verified information | Appropriate use in the dissertation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Union Bank of India Integrated Annual Report 2023–24, p. 227 | The Learning and Development unit reported the launch of **Union Vidya** as the Bank’s Learning Management System. The report describes e-books, podcasts and audiobooks, social-learning platforms, microlearning modules, and virtual workshops as resources integrated in Union Vidya. It also reports trainer-led classroom and locational programmes delivered through the wider training system. | Establishes that Union Vidya is the Bank’s LMS and that digital resources sit alongside trainer-led learning activity. |
| Union Bank of India Integrated Annual Report 2024–25, p. 128 | The Bank reported a Master Policy on Learning and Development covering e-learning, virtual training, and staggered programmes. It reported ten mandatory e-learning modules for officers, role-based training modules, and the launch of a Training Management System within Union Vidya as a one-stop digital platform. | Establishes current organisational infrastructure for digital learning, structured pathways, and training administration. |

## Research Interpretation and Boundary

The reports show that Union Bank has an LMS and uses several learning-delivery methods. This makes the Bank a relevant setting for a study of hybrid learning. The reports do **not** establish that every programme combines digital and face-to-face learning, that every employee uses the same Union Vidya features, or that the LMS caused better learning or performance.

The present study therefore asks eligible employees about one recent programme that included both digital and face-to-face learning. Union Vidya is treated as the digital-learning context within the **Hybrid Learning** construct. It is not a separate technology-quality variable, and the questionnaire does not evaluate system uptime, technical support, analytics, or Bank-wide training outcomes.

## Use in Version 3

- **Chapter 1:** explains Union Vidya and the wider Bank learning environment.
- **Chapter 2:** situates the Union Bank case and distinguishes documented infrastructure from employee perceptions that the survey will examine.
- **Chapter 3 and questionnaire:** retain platform access, navigation, material availability, and revision access as elements of Hybrid Learning, without assuming every programme uses the same platform.
