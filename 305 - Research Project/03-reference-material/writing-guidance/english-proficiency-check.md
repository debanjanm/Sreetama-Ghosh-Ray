# English Proficiency Check — for Sreetama

**Purpose.** General-purpose English proficiency check — not tied to the thesis or any one topic. It measures baseline grammar, vocabulary, and register accuracy on its own, independent of subject matter. Separate from the [voice questionnaire](voice-questions-for-sreetama.md), which captures *how you naturally write* on thesis-specific topics. This one calibrates *baseline accuracy* generally, so the rewrite skill (or any future writing help) can tell the difference between "this is just how Sreetama phrases things" (keep it) and "this is a slip she'd actually want corrected" (fix it) — for the thesis or anything else she writes — instead of guessing from open-text answers alone.

**How to answer:** Pick one option per question, no looking things up, no AI. Guessing is fine — the point is your actual instinct, not a perfect score. Takes about 10 minutes.

**Consent:** Only Sreetama should answer this. Used only to build her own writing-style skill.

---

### A. Grammar

1. Choose the correct sentence.
   a) The result of the two studies were surprising.
   b) The result of the two studies was surprising.
   c) The results of the study was surprising.
   d) The result of the study have been surprising.

2. Choose the correct sentence.
   a) Money does not significantly differ people's happiness.
   b) Money does not significantly affect people's happiness.
   c) Money does not significant affect people's happiness.
   d) Money not significantly affects people's happiness.

3. Choose the correct sentence.
   a) The manager wanted to access the situation before deciding.
   b) The manager wanted to assess the situation before deciding.
   c) The manager wanted to assess the situations's before deciding.
   d) The manager wanted for assessing of the situation before deciding.

4. Choose the correct sentence.
   a) Since 2020, remote work have become far more common.
   b) Since 2020, remote work has become far more common.
   c) Since 2020, remote work becoming far more common.
   d) Since 2020, remote work become far more common already.

5. Choose the correct sentence.
   a) Each of the students were given a certificate.
   b) Each of the students was given a certificate.
   c) Each of the student were given certificates.
   d) Each students was given a certificate.

6. Choose the correct sentence.
   a) The decision is based on last year's data.
   b) The decision is basing on last year's data.
   c) The decision bases in last year's data.
   d) The decision is based off last year's data.

7. Choose the correct preposition: "The new policy resulted ___ a noticeable drop in complaints."
   a) to
   b) with
   c) in
   d) for

8. Choose the correct sentence.
   a) There is a significant difference between the two group.
   b) There is significant difference between the two groups.
   c) There is a significant difference between the two groups.
   d) There are a significant difference between two groups.

### C. Register recognition — pick the one that fits formal/academic writing

12. Which sentence fits a formal report's findings section?
    a) Basically, most people said the new system was pretty good, tbh.
    b) The findings indicate that a majority of respondents rated the system favourably.
    c) Like, the system was rated good by most people lol.
    d) So yeah, the system got good ratings from most.

13. Which sentence fits a formal report's background/introduction section?
    a) Companies these days are obsessed with digital tools, ngl.
    b) In our company, we really care about doing things well.
    c) Organisations have increasingly adopted digital tools in response to changing customer expectations.
    d) Digital tools are a big deal now, everyone knows that.

14. Which closing sentence fits an academic or formal conclusion?
    a) So that's basically it, hope it made sense.
    b) The findings underscore the strategic importance of a well-designed onboarding process.
    c) Anyway, that's my conclusion, thanks for reading.
    d) In our opinion, onboarding is good and companies should do it.

15. Which sentence correctly uses third-person academic register (no "I"/"we")?
    a) I found that leadership support mattered a lot to employees.
    b) We think leadership support really matters for employees.
    c) The findings suggest that leadership support is an important factor for employees.
    d) In our study, we feel leadership support is important.

**How this gets used:** a high score on A/B (grammar/vocabulary) means her open-answer phrasing choices in the voice questionnaire are more likely *deliberate style*, not accidental — the rewrite skill can preserve more of her unusual constructions as-is. A lower score flags specific categories (from Q17 too) where the skill should quietly clean up genuine errors rather than "preserving voice" that's actually just a slip. Register questions (C) calibrate how naturally she already reads formal register, separate from grammar accuracy.
