Yes. I checked the uploaded refined version carefully. 

**My assessment: it is much more natural and respondent-friendly than the earlier version, but I would make a few important research-quality changes before treating it as the final questionnaire.**

### What is already good

* The language is simple enough for bank employees across different age and job groups.
* The progression **Hybrid Learning → Training Effectiveness → Knowledge Retention → Job Performance** is easy to follow.
* The items are mostly specific rather than abstract.
* The open-ended questions in Section F are useful for collecting supporting qualitative comments.
* The questionnaire does **not read like obvious AI-generated academic prose**. It sounds conversational and respondent-oriented.

### What I would change

**1. Avoid overly conversational wording in the actual measurement items.**

For example:

> “I like that I can choose when…”

and

> “I walk away from the training…”

and

> “the training has genuinely improved…”

These sound natural, but they introduce **attitude/emotion or informal phrasing** that is not ideal for a master's research instrument.

A better balance is **simple + natural + academically neutral**.

---

**2. HL2 is double-barrelled.**

> “Mixing online and classroom sessions makes it easier for me to fit training around my work and personal schedule.”

This combines two things: the *combination of modes* and *work/personal schedule*. Respondents may agree with one but not the other.

I'd use:

> **HL2. The combination of online and classroom sessions makes it easier for me to manage my training time.**

---

**3. HL13 is good conceptually but slightly informal.**

> “feel like one continuous programme — not two separate things.”

This is actually measuring **integration**, which is important, but the wording should be more neutral:

> **HL13. The online and classroom sessions are well connected as part of the same training programme.**

That is much easier to defend to your research guide.

---

**4. TE9 should be changed.**

Current:

> “Because of the training, I feel more confident handling work situations that used to stress me out.”

“Stress me out” introduces an emotional/psychological dimension that you haven't defined as a construct.

Use:

> **TE9. The training has increased my confidence in handling work-related situations.**

---

**5. KR7 is too colloquial.**

> “The important things I learned in training stick with me for a good amount of time.”

Use:

> **KR7. I retain important knowledge from training for a reasonable period of time.**

---

**6. JP6 may not measure job performance cleanly.**

> “I feel more in control of my workload and responsibilities…”

This can depend on workload, staffing and management—not necessarily training.

A stronger item is:

> **JP6. Training has helped me manage my work responsibilities more effectively.**

---

**7. Be careful with causal wording.**

This is particularly important for your research.

Statements such as:

> “Because of what I learned…”

> “since attending the training…”

> “training has made me better…”

implicitly establish causality.

For a master's questionnaire, it is safer to measure the respondent's **perceived contribution of training**, unless your research design is specifically causal.

### One methodological concern I would address before data collection

Your **Section B is quite strong**, particularly B5. The distinction between *having online + classroom training* and *actually integrating those two modes* is important.

However, **Sections C, D and E need a clear conceptual justification**. At present:

* **Training Effectiveness** = relevance + learning + application
* **Knowledge Retention** = remembering/recalling
* **Job Performance** = work outcomes

That is workable, but you need to explain **why retention and job performance are separate from training effectiveness** in your conceptual framework.

I would **not add more variables yet**. Your questionnaire already has **51 Likert-scale items**, which is substantial for a master's study. The priority now should be **construct validity and theoretical justification**, not adding more questions.
