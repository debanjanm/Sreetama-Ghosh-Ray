## Introduction to Respondents

This questionnaire is part of a master's research study on hybrid learning and training effectiveness at Union Bank of India. Hybrid learning refers to a combination of digital/online training and face-to-face/classroom training. There are no right or wrong answers; please respond based on your actual experience. Responses will be used only for academic purposes and treated confidentially.

## Response Scale

1 – Strongly Disagree · 2 – Disagree · 3 – Neither Agree nor Disagree · 4 – Agree · 5 – Strongly Agree

## Section A: Basic Information

1. Age group: Below 25 / 25–34 / 35–44 / 45–54 / 55 and above
2. Years of work experience: Less than 5 / 5–10 / 11–15 / 16–20 / More than 20
3. Job level: Clerical / Officer / Managerial / Other
4. How often have you attended training in the last 12 months? None / 1–2 programmes / 3–4 programmes / 5 or more
5. How much of your recent training was delivered digitally/online? None / <25% / 25–50% / 51–75% / >75%
6. How much of your recent training was delivered face-to-face/classroom? None / <25% / 25–50% / 51–75% / >75%

## Section B: Hybrid Learning

HL1. I can choose when to go through the online parts of the training.

HL4. I can easily find and access the online training materials.

HL7. I get sufficient opportunities to interact with the trainer and ask questions during the training.

HL10. The digital platform or application used for training is easy to navigate and use.

HL11. Technical problems do not usually interfere with my learning.

HL12. The online content helps me understand the topics covered in the training.

HL13. The online and classroom sessions are well connected as part of the same training programme.

HL14. The classroom sessions build on what I learned during the online sessions.

## Section C: Trainer Competence

TC1. The trainer/facilitator has strong knowledge of the training subject matter.

TC3. The trainer is comfortable using the digital tools and platforms involved in the training.

TC7. The trainer motivates me to actively participate in the training.

## Section D: Learner Engagement

EN1. I stay fully engaged and attentive during the training sessions, whether online or in-person.

EN2. I feel absorbed in the training activities rather than simply going through the motions.

EN3. I actively participate in discussions, exercises, or activities during the training.

EN4. I put genuine effort into understanding the training content, not just completing it.

## Section E: Training Effectiveness

TE1. The content covered in the training is relevant to the work I do.

TE4. The training helps me understand the topics covered clearly.

TE5. I gain useful knowledge from the training.

TE7. The training provides knowledge and skills that I can use in my day-to-day work.

TE8. I am able to apply the skills learned during training to my job.

## Section F: Knowledge Retention

KR1. I can still recall the main points covered during the training.

KR2. Even weeks or months later, I can remember the key concepts covered in the training.

KR4. I can explain the main ideas from the training in my own words.

KR7. I retain important knowledge from training for a reasonable period of time.

KR9. Revision or refresher materials help me remember what I learned.

## Section G: Job Performance

JP1. The knowledge and skills gained from training help me perform my work more accurately.

JP2. Training has helped improve the overall quality of my work.

JP4. Training helps me complete my work more efficiently.

JP7. I regularly apply what I learned during training in my job.

JP9. Overall, the training has improved my job performance.

## Optional Final Questions

1. What do you like most about hybrid training?
2. What are the main problems you face in hybrid training?
3. What changes would make hybrid training more effective in your bank?
