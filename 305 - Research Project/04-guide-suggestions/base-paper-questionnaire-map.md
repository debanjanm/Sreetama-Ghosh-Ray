# Base Paper to Questionnaire Map

## Base Paper

Uddin, M., Ahamed, M. R., Jakowan, Islam, M. R., & Nahar, M. (2026). *How Blended Learning Influences Employee Performance: The Mediating Role of Soft Skill Development and Knowledge Acquisition.* *International Journal of Accounting and Economics Studies, 13*(2), 243–250. https://doi.org/10.14419/9cjynm83

The paper provides the workplace blended-learning-to-performance logic used in this study:

```text
Blended Learning Effectiveness
→ Soft-Skill Development and Knowledge Acquisition
→ Employee Performance
```

## Questionnaire Adaptation

The questionnaire is inspired by this pathway and adapted for employees of Union Bank of India. It retains the central idea that an integrated digital and in-person training experience supports work-related learning and, in turn, employee performance.

| Base-paper logic | Present-study construct | Questionnaire questions | Purpose in the present study |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Blended Learning Effectiveness | Hybrid Learning | 5–11 | Measures employees’ experience of flexibility, access, interaction, technology, and integration of online and classroom training. |
| Knowledge Acquisition | Training Effectiveness | 19–22 | Measures employees’ immediate understanding, usefulness, relevance, and job applicability of training. |
| Knowledge Acquisition over time | Knowledge Retention | 23–26 | Extends the learning pathway by measuring recall and availability of training content in later work situations. |
| Employee Performance | Job Performance | 27–30 | Measures employees’ perceived accuracy, quality, efficiency, and application of learning in their work. |

Trainer Competence (Questions 12–14) and Learner Engagement (Questions 15–18) extend the base-paper pathway for the bank setting. They explain the conditions through which a hybrid learning experience may become effective training and retained knowledge.

```text
Hybrid Learning + Trainer Competence
→ Learner Engagement
→ Training Effectiveness
→ Knowledge Retention
→ Job Performance
```

The questionnaire therefore follows the base paper’s core workplace blended-learning-to-performance logic while adapting the variables to the approved six-construct model.
