# Hybrid Learning and Training Effectiveness

This is the fresh Version 2 dissertation area for the study **Hybrid Learning and Training Effectiveness at Union Bank of India**.

## Study Model

```text
Hybrid Learning → Training Effectiveness
```

Hybrid Learning is the independent variable. Training Effectiveness is the dependent variable. LMS experience, integration of digital and in-person learning, access, flexibility, trainer guidance, and interaction are dimensions of Hybrid Learning. Learning, job relevance, transfer, and individual work improvement are dimensions of Training Effectiveness.
