# Hybrid Learning, Trainer Competence, Learner Engagement and Training Effectiveness

This is Version 3 of the dissertation area for the study **Hybrid Learning and Training Effectiveness at Union Bank of India**. It is a separate four-construct draft. The earlier six-construct study in [01-current-thesis](../01-current-thesis/) and the earlier two-construct study in [01-current-thesis-v2](../01-current-thesis-v2/) remain unchanged for comparison.

## Study Model

    Hybrid Learning ─┐
                     ├──→ Learner Engagement ───→ Training Effectiveness
    Trainer Competence ┘

Hybrid Learning and Trainer Competence are the two antecedent constructs. Learner Engagement is the immediate learning-process construct. Training Effectiveness is the outcome construct.

The study does not claim a formal causal mediation effect. It uses a cross-sectional employee survey to examine relationships between these constructs.

The study uses three complementary base papers. Sosnova et al. (2025) provides the guide-suggested conceptual link between hybrid learning and learning effectiveness. Kim (2022) provides corporate evidence on hybrid training and training effectiveness. Saroj, Sahney and Sekar provide the workplace evidence on instructor competence, learner engagement, and blended learning. Their distinct roles are documented in the base-paper rationale.
