4.2 Inferential Analysis
4.2.1 Correlation Analysis
Pearson’s r indicates the direction and the strength of each relationship between pairs of variables. Every relationship shown in Table 4.6 is positive and has statistical significance.
Learner Engagement has the strongest pairwise connection with Training Effectiveness (r = .765): people who gave higher ratings for their involvement also generally gave higher ratings for how effective the training was. There is a strong connection between hybrid learning and the competence of trainers, as indicated by a correlation coefficient of .818. The connection between them is important when understanding the individual regression coefficients.
4.2.2 Multiple Regression Analysis
The main hypothesis employs multiple regression analysis, where Training Effectiveness is the dependent variable, and the other three constructs are included as predictors in the same model.
The model is significant, with an F-value of 50.652 and a p-value less than .001. One thing that stands out is how the three predictors together explain 60.8 percent of the variation in the reported Training Effectiveness across the 102 cases. This supports the overall alternative hypothesis.
When all three predictors are included in the model, only Learner Engagement has a statistically significant coefficient (β = .532, p < .001). When considering the overlap with Hybrid Learning and Trainer Competence, higher effectiveness ratings are linked to higher engagement ratings.
In this combined model, both Hybrid Learning and Trainer Competence show positive but not statistically significant individual effects. Their correlations with each other stay positive, including a strong link between them. For these respondents, Learner Engagement demonstrates the most distinct statistical relationship when all three variables are considered together; the other two variables should not be considered unimportant simply because of this.
One thing that was not included in the saved regression results is the VIF and tolerance values. The interpretation therefore recognizes the significant relationship between Hybrid Learning and Trainer Competence as demonstrated in Table 4.6.

4.3 Interpretation of Results
Employees who felt more involved in the chosen program also provided higher scores for how relevant it was, how confident they felt, and how useful it was for their work. For bank employees, participation might involve closely following a session, asking questions, practicing a procedure, or reviewing the material again when a task comes up.
