5.8 Conclusion
The general assumption that there is no relationship between the predictors and perceived Training Effectiveness is rejected based on the 102 valid cases.The three predictors together have a significant relationship with perceived Training Effectiveness, as indicated by an R squared value of 0.608. The alternative hypothesis is supported by the combined model. When other factors are already considered, learner engagement still has a strong individual impact.
For Union Bank, the results suggest the need to examine their preparation and consider possibilities for active involvement. Digital resources and instructor-guided activities should be clearly related to the tasks that employees perform. These suggestions are based on the observed patterns; the analysis does not indicate that applying them would lead to improved results.
