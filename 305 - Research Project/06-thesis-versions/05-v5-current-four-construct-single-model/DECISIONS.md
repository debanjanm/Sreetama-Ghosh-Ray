# Version 5 Decision Record

## Purpose

Version 5 replaces the earlier chain model with one multiple-regression model. It is a separate version and does not overwrite Version 4.

## Final Model

```text
Hybrid Learning ────────┐
Trainer Competence ─────┼──→ Training Effectiveness
Learner Engagement ────┘
```

Hybrid Learning, Trainer Competence, and Learner Engagement are separate independent variables. Training Effectiveness is the dependent variable. The model does not present Learner Engagement as a mediator.

## Reason for Retaining Learner Engagement

Learner Engagement remains a separate predictor because employees may engage differently even when a programme is well planned and the trainer is competent. Workload, time availability, motivation, prior experience, digital confidence, and workplace conditions may also affect employee involvement during training.

## Analysis Source and Sample

Version 5 uses the supplied SPSS Statistics 23 output as its result source. The output reports 102 valid regression cases. It shows one case excluded through SPSS listwise handling because Trainer Competence mean data were missing in the SPSS analysis file. The original 103-record dataset and all raw collection records remain preserved outside Version 5.

The SPSS output alone does not identify a defensible raw-record deletion. Therefore, Version 5 reports the 102 valid SPSS cases without naming an individual respondent in thesis text.

## Instrument

The approved questionnaire remains unchanged: 30 numbered questions, 26 Likert-scale items, and four scored constructs. The model changes how the construct scores are analysed; it does not alter what respondents were asked.
