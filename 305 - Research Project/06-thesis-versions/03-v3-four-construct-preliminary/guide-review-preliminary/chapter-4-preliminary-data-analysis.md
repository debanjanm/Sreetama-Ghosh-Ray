## 4.3 Construct Descriptives and Reliability

Each construct score is an arithmetic mean on the original 1-5 scale. A mean above 3 indicates average agreement.

| Construct | Items | Mean | Standard deviation | Cronbach's alpha |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Hybrid Learning | 7 | 3.89 | 1.25 | .987 |
| Trainer Competence | 6 | 3.91 | 1.30 | .987 |
| Learner Engagement | 6 | 3.92 | 1.35 | .989 |
| Training Effectiveness | 7 | 3.64 | 1.40 | .990 |

Learner Engagement had the highest interim mean. Training Effectiveness had the lowest mean but was still above the neutral score of 3. The alpha values are extremely high. They show that responses moved together in the first 22 records, but do not prove full validation of the instrument. The small sample and same-answer patterns can increase alpha.

## 4.8 Interpretation Boundary

The results describe only the first 22 eligible respondents. Small sample size, non-probability sampling, self-report data, same-answer patterns, and high predictor overlap limit interpretation. The tables show how the answers are derived. They are not final dissertation findings.
