# Chapter 5 — Illustrative Conclusion Template Only

This folder is reserved for the final Chapter 5 after real data collection and analysis.

The final chapter should answer the three research questions, state whether each relationship hypothesis is supported, discuss the findings against the three base papers, give practical recommendations for Union Bank training design, and acknowledge the limits of a cross-sectional self-report survey.

Where the actual findings support a need for improved programme integration, the recommendations may use a five-stage implementation lens: Prepare, Engage, Practice, Apply, and Reinforce. This means digital preparation before a programme where appropriate, trainer-led engagement and practice, opportunity to apply relevant learning at work, and later digital reinforcement or revision. It is a practical recommendation framework only. It is not an additional research model, variable set, questionnaire section, or pre-written conclusion, and it must be applied only in a way that is supported by the completed survey findings.
