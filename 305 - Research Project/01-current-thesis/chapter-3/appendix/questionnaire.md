# Appendix — Questionnaire

## Hybrid Learning and Training Effectiveness at Union Bank of India

This questionnaire is part of an academic study on hybrid learning and training effectiveness at Union Bank of India. Hybrid learning refers to a training programme that combines digital or online learning with face-to-face or classroom learning.

Please answer every statement with reference to your **most recent hybrid-training programme attended during the last 12 months**. There are no right or wrong answers. Your responses are confidential, voluntary, and used only for academic purposes. Do not write your name or employee number anywhere on this questionnaire.

### Eligibility and Basic Information

1. During the last 12 months, have you attended at least one training programme that included both digital or online learning and face-to-face or classroom learning?  
   Yes / No  
   *If your answer is No, please do not continue with the questionnaire.*

3. Years of work experience: Less than 5 / 5–10 / 11–15 / 16–20 / More than 20

4. Job level: Clerical / Officer / Managerial / Other

1 – Strongly Disagree · 2 – Disagree · 3 – Neither Agree nor Disagree · 4 – Agree · 5 – Strongly Agree
