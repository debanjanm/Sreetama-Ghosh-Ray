# Union Vidya LMS Context — Official Source Record

## Purpose

This record documents the official Union Bank of India information used to describe the local learning environment in Version 4. It supports organisational context only. It is not a research paper, a measurement scale, or evidence that every employee has the same learning experience.

## Verified Organisational Facts

## Research Interpretation and Boundary

The official reports, public Bank sources, and the internal circular show that Union Bank has an LMS and uses several learning-delivery methods. This makes the Bank a relevant setting for a study of hybrid learning. The reports do **not** establish that every programme combines digital and face-to-face learning, that every employee uses the same Union Vidya features, or that the LMS caused better learning or performance.

The present study therefore asks eligible employees about one recent programme that included both digital and face-to-face learning. Union Vidya is treated as the digital-learning context within the **Hybrid Learning** construct. It is not a separate technology-quality variable, and the questionnaire does not evaluate system uptime, technical support, analytics, or Bank-wide training outcomes.

## Use in Version 4

- **Chapter 1:** explains the banking context, brief Union Bank history, Union Vidya, and the wider Bank learning environment.
- **Chapter 2:** situates the Union Bank case and distinguishes documented delivery arrangements from employee perceptions that the survey will examine.
- **Chapter 3 and questionnaire:** retain platform access, navigation, material availability, and revision access as elements of Hybrid Learning, without assuming every programme uses the same platform.
