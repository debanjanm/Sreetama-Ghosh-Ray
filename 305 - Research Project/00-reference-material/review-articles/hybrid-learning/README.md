# Hybrid Learning Article Collection

This collection supports the independent variable: **Hybrid Learning**. In this dissertation, hybrid learning means a planned training experience in which digital learning and face-to-face learning are connected as parts of the same programme.

## Source Use

| Source | Type | Use in Version 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Uddin et al. (2026) | Workplace journal article | Corporate blended-learning-to-performance relationship; conceptual starting point. |
| Kumar and Pande (2017) | Workplace journal article | Working-professional context; integration, technology, learner needs, and LMS-related success factors. |
| Mulaudzi (2021) | Open-access workplace dissertation | Corporate questionnaire wording ideas on access, connectivity, time management, and blended delivery. |
| Han and Ellis (2020) | Scale-development journal article | Structure of perceived blended-learning environment: integration, online contribution, and online workload. |
| Union Bank of India (2024, 2025) | Official annual reports | Local LMS context: Union Vidya, digital resources, e-learning, virtual and staggered training, role-based learning, and the Training Management System. |
