# Rapid Viva Revision Sheet

# Hybrid Learning and Training Effectiveness at Union Bank of India

## Study in one sentence

I studied whether employees who report better connected Hybrid Learning and stronger Trainer Competence also report higher Learner Engagement, and whether engaged employees report greater Training Effectiveness.

## Model

```text
Hybrid Learning ─┐
                 ├──→ Learner Engagement ───→ Training Effectiveness
Trainer Competence ┘
```

**Important:** This tests associations. It does not prove causation or formal mediation.

## Why this study matters

- Banking employees must learn procedures, compliance, products, digital systems, and customer-service practices while working.
- Union Bank has digital and trainer-led learning infrastructure, including Union Vidya.
- Organisational infrastructure does not show how employees experience one programme.
- Gap: limited evidence combining all four constructs in a public-sector Indian bank context.

## Three base papers

- **Sosnova et al. (2025):** conceptual Hybrid Learning and learning-effectiveness anchor; student setting.
- **Kim (2022):** corporate hybrid-training evidence; design, instructor involvement, practice, and effectiveness.
- **Bahl et al. (2024):** Indian banking Training Effectiveness anchor; 402 bank employees; Kirkpatrick framework.

## Variables and items

| Construct | Meaning | Items |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Hybrid Learning | Connected digital and face-to-face learning; platform access and revision | 7 |
| Trainer Competence | Knowledge, clarity, digital facilitation, feedback, participation support | 6 |
| Learner Engagement | Attention, effort, participation, interest, clarification-seeking | 6 |
| Training Effectiveness | Understanding, relevance, capability, application, confidence | 7 |

**Questionnaire:** 30 questions = 4 eligibility/profile + 26 Likert items.
**Scale:** 1 Strongly Disagree to 5 Strongly Agree.
**Union Vidya:** LMS context within Hybrid Learning; not a separate variable.

## Collection and sample

- Chennai South Regional Office, associated locations, and branches.
- 70 paper questionnaires + 62 Google Forms = **132 received**.
- **105 retained:** 56 paper + 49 online.
- **27 excluded:** 16 said No to hybrid-training eligibility; 11 paper forms had unclear/blank eligibility.
- Sampling: purposive, because only employees with relevant hybrid-training experience could answer.

## Data cleaning

- Retained only Q1 = Yes.
- Checked 26 scored items were present and within 1–5.
- Two documented imputed values only:
  - Manual-25 HL6 = **3.333**
  - Manual-46 HL4 = **4.000**
- Similar anonymous patterns were retained because similarity alone does not prove duplicate respondents.

## Main statistics

### Reliability: Cronbach’s alpha

| HL | TC | LE | TE |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| .961 | .969 | .958 | .971 |

High internal consistency. It does not prove full instrument validity.

### Hypothesis results

| Hypothesis | Result |
| --- | --- |
| H1: HL ↔ LE | r = .715, p < .001, supported |
| H2: TC ↔ LE | r = .726, p < .001, supported |
| H3: LE ↔ TE | r = .765, p < .001, supported |

### Regression results

- HL + TC → LE: **R² = .572**, F(2,102) = 68.218, p < .001.
- LE → TE: **R² = .585**, F(1,103) = 145.452, p < .001.
- VIF for HL and TC = **3.005**; overlap exists but is below the common screening value of 5.

## Five recommendations

1. Make the sequence between digital learning, trainer session, and follow-up clear.
2. Keep Union Vidya or other platform material easy to find and revisit.
3. Support trainers to connect the two modes, invite participation, and give feedback.
4. Include structured questions, case discussion, practice, and follow-up clarification.
5. Use job-related examples, checklists, or application prompts after training.

## Limitations to say openly

- Purposive Chennai sample; cannot statistically represent all Union Bank employees.
- Cross-sectional data; cannot prove cause and effect.
- Same respondents reported all constructs; common self-report method may strengthen associations.
- No separate pilot study.
- No examination scores, system logs, supervisor ratings, customer outcomes, or Bank-level performance data.
- SPSS syntax is prepared for reproduction; only claim SPSS-generated output when it is retained.

## Best final answer

> My study provides focused employee feedback on hybrid training in the Chennai Union Bank context. It found positive relationships among programme connection, trainer support, engagement, and perceived training effectiveness. The results guide review of training design, but they do not prove that any one change will cause better Bank-wide performance.
