# Base Paper Selection

## Selected Paper

Uddin, M., Ahamed, M. R., Jakowan, Islam, M. R., & Nahar, M. (2026). *How Blended Learning Influences Employee Performance: The Mediating Role of Soft Skill Development and Knowledge Acquisition.* *International Journal of Accounting and Economics Studies, 13*(2), 243–250. https://doi.org/10.14419/9cjynm83

## Why It Is the Base Paper

The paper examines corporate employees with prior blended-training experience. It treats blended learning as the combination of in-person sessions and digital learning tools, and it tests a clear pathway to employee performance:

```text
Blended Learning Effectiveness
→ Soft-Skill Development and Knowledge Acquisition
→ Employee Performance
```

This dissertation adopts that workplace blended-learning-to-performance logic. It extends the model for Union Bank of India by adding Trainer Competence and Learner Engagement before Training Effectiveness, and by examining Knowledge Retention as the link between Training Effectiveness and Job Performance.

```text
Hybrid Learning + Trainer Competence
→ Learner Engagement
→ Training Effectiveness
→ Knowledge Retention
→ Job Performance
```

The paper is the conceptual base. Saroj, Sahney and Sekar (2026) remains the primary supporting source for the Trainer Competence and Learner Engagement relationship. Agarwal et al. (2026) supports the hybrid-training-to-performance relationship in the Indian financial-services context. Mulaudzi (2021) remains a workplace source for questionnaire wording and practical delivery issues.
