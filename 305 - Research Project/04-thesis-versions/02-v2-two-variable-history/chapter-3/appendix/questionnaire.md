# Appendix — Questionnaire

## Hybrid Learning and Training Effectiveness at Union Bank of India

This questionnaire forms part of an academic study on hybrid learning and training effectiveness among employees of Union Bank of India. In this study, hybrid learning means a training programme that combines digital learning with face-to-face learning as connected parts of the same programme.

Please answer the statements with reference to **one hybrid-training programme that you attended during the last 12 months**. The digital component may have used Union Vidya or another authorized learning platform. There are no right or wrong answers. Your participation is voluntary, and your answers are confidential. Do not write your name or employee number on this questionnaire.

## Eligibility and Basic Information

1. During the last 12 months, have you attended at least one training programme that included both digital or online learning and face-to-face learning?  
   Yes / No  
   *If your answer is No, please do not continue with the questionnaire.*

2. Age group: Below 25 / 25–34 / 35–44 / 45–54 / 55 and above

3. Length of service in Union Bank of India: Below 5 years / 5–10 years / 11–20 years / Above 20 years

4. Job level: Clerical / Officer / Managerial / Other
