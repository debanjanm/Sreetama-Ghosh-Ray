# Questionnaire Evidence Map

## Purpose

This map records the literature basis for the Version 2 questionnaire. The instrument is designed for employees of Union Bank of India and asks about one recent hybrid-training programme. It is not presented as a verbatim reproduction of any one published scale.

The questionnaire measures two broad variables:

```text
Hybrid Learning → Training Effectiveness
```

The wording is adapted to a banking-training context and is reviewed through a pilot study before the main survey.

## Hybrid Learning: Questions 5–17

| Dimension | Questions | Evidence basis | Adaptation decision |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Integration of modes | 5–7 | Garrison and Kanuka (2004); Han and Ellis (2020); Kumar and Pande (2017) | Items ask whether online and face-to-face parts form one connected programme, rather than asking about separate formats. |
| LMS access and usability | 8–11 | Kumar and Pande (2017); Mulaudzi (2021); Union Bank of India (2025) | Items refer to the digital platform used for the programme. The wording names Union Vidya only as an example because programme platforms may differ. |
| Flexibility and learning content | 12–13 | Kumar and Pande (2017); Mulaudzi (2021) | Items cover suitable timing and the ability to revisit digital material. |
| Interaction and trainer guidance | 14–17 | Boelens, De Wever and Voet (2017); Kumar and Pande (2017); Mulaudzi (2021) | Items treat trainer guidance and interaction as qualities of hybrid delivery, not as a separate tested variable. |

## Training Effectiveness: Questions 18–30

| Dimension | Questions | Evidence basis | Adaptation decision |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Learning and understanding | 18–21 | Kirkpatrick (1994); Aziz (2015) | Items assess useful understanding and ability to use the knowledge or skill taught. |
| Work relevance | 22–23 | Baldwin and Ford (1988); Uddin et al. (2026) | Items ask whether the training relates to actual work needs and work problems. |
| Transfer and application | 24–26 | Baldwin and Ford (1988); Blume et al. (2010); Aziz (2015) | Items assess use of learning in daily work and confidence in applying it. |
| Individual work improvement | 27–30 | Aziz (2015); Uddin et al. (2026) | Items measure perceived accuracy, efficiency, work quality, and confidence. Organization-wide productivity and reputation are intentionally excluded because individual employees cannot reliably judge them. |

## Item-Development Note

Han and Ellis (2020) provides a validated structure for perceived blended-learning environments, but its student wording is not used unchanged. Aziz (2015) provides a validated workplace Training Effectiveness Scale, but its wording is adapted for a Union Bank employee survey and the organization-level items are excluded. Kumar and Pande (2017), Mulaudzi (2021), and Union Bank of India (2025) support the corporate learning, LMS, and delivery context.

The final instrument is therefore a **literature-informed, context-adapted questionnaire**. Content validity is strengthened through supervisor review and a pilot with eligible employees. Reliability is assessed separately for the Hybrid Learning and Training Effectiveness item sets through Cronbach's alpha.
