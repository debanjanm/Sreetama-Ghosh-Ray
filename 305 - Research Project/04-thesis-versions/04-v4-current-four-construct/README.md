# Version 4 — Hybrid Learning and Training Effectiveness at Union Bank of India

Version 4 is a separate, voice-led rewrite of the approved four-construct study. It preserves the study model, hypotheses, evidence base, base-paper roles, questionnaire, and analytical boundaries established in Version 3. Its prose is rewritten in Sreetama Ghosh Ray’s practical academic voice: formal, evidence-led, and attentive to the realities of employee learning in a public-sector bank.

## Study model

    Hybrid Learning ─┐
                     ├──→ Learner Engagement ───→ Training Effectiveness
    Trainer Competence ┘

The study examines reported relationships in a cross-sectional employee survey. It does not claim formal mediation or causation.
