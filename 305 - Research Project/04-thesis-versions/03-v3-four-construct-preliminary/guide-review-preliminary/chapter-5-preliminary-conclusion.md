# Chapter 5 - Conclusion and Next Steps
## Preliminary Guide-Review Version

> Status: This is an interim discussion of 22 eligible responses. It does not replace the final dissertation conclusion.

## 5.1 Preliminary Conclusion

The first 22 eligible respondents generally reported positive views of the selected hybrid-training programme. The interim means were 3.89 for Hybrid Learning, 3.91 for Trainer Competence, 3.92 for Learner Engagement, and 3.64 for Training Effectiveness. Training Effectiveness was the lowest mean, but it remained above the neutral response point.

Employees who reported a more positive hybrid-learning experience tended to report higher engagement. Employees who reported stronger trainer competence also tended to report higher engagement. Engagement was positively associated with reported training effectiveness.

These observations are useful for guide discussion but are not final findings. The dataset has only 22 eligible records and five same-answer patterns. Data collection should continue before making a final statement about the selected Union Bank employee group.

## 5.2 Meaning of the Interim Pattern

The pattern fits the study logic. Connected digital and face-to-face activity may help employees participate in the programme. Clear explanation, encouragement, feedback, and the ability to connect learning modes may also matter to engagement.

Hybrid Learning and Trainer Competence overlap almost completely in the current data. The multiple regression cannot distinguish their separate relationship with engagement. The appropriate conclusion is not that one is more important than the other. A larger and more varied sample is required before comparing their unique contribution.

The Engagement-Training Effectiveness relationship is clearer. Respondents who reported more attention, effort, participation, involvement, clarification-seeking, and interest also reported more understanding, relevance, capability, application, task support, and confidence. This is compatible with the workplace-training concerns raised by Kim (2022) and the employee-level banking training evaluation described by Bahl, Kiran and Sharma (2024). It does not prove that engagement produced the reported outcome.

## 5.3 Implications for the Final Study

1. Retain the four separate construct scores.
2. Recheck the overlap between Hybrid Learning and Trainer Competence.
3. Interpret reliability with item distributions and response-pattern checks.
4. Route a No eligibility response in the Google Form directly to the end of the form.
5. Keep final recommendations limited to employee-level learning experience.

## 5.4 Limitations and Next Steps

The interim sample uses non-probability responses and cannot represent all Union Bank employees. The same respondent reports programme experience, trainer competence, engagement, and effectiveness at one point in time. The design cannot establish causation or formal mediation.

The five same-answer patterns are retained because removing them without evidence would be unsupported. Their presence and the resulting very high reliability and correlation values must be stated openly.

Data collection should continue until approximately 80 eligible respondents are reached. The complete export should then be preserved, screened, coded using the same rules, and reanalysed. The final Chapters 4 and 5 should replace this preliminary guide-review version and report associations rather than causal effects.
