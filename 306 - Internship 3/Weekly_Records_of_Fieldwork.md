**Total: 40 hrs**

#### Learnings and Key Takeaways
- A merger cannot be managed by a one-time orientation. Employees from different banks need continuous training, updates and chances to clear doubts in order to adapt to common systems and SOPs.
- Training is an important part of change management. It helps employees understand why a change is being made and apply revised procedures with confidence.
- Digital learning gives flexibility when staff cannot leave the branch, while classroom and interactive sessions remain valuable for discussion, demonstration and clarification. A balanced hybrid approach suits banking best.
- Training effectiveness depends on whether employees can retain and apply the learning at work, not just on attending a programme or completing a module.
