## Slide 2 — Organisation and Office Profile

- **Union Bank of India:** a public-sector bank with Head/Central Office in Mumbai, operating through Zonal, Regional and Branch levels.
- **Regional Office Chennai South, Royapettah:** administers approximately 75 branches — welfare, attendance, leave, transfers, salary and allowance processing.
- **HR Department at the RO:** the link between Head Office policy and day-to-day branch administration; also supports training, industrial relations and disciplinary matters.

---

## Slide 3 — Internship Scope

- **Objective:** to gain practical exposure to HR operations in a public-sector bank, alongside primary data collection for the dissertation on Hybrid Learning and Training Effectiveness.
- **Divisional Exposure:** involved in orientation across the RO's HR desk, the Rural and Agriculture Business Department (RABD), Currency Chest, and the branch network.
- **Four Main Work Areas:**
  - HR administration and leave management
  - Transfers and disciplinary procedure
  - Branch visits and primary data collection
  - Understanding change management after the bank amalgamation

---

## Slide 4 — HR Administration at the Regional Office

- **Daily Circulars:** morning prayer followed by reading of circulars on performance, NPAs, policy updates and training requirements.
- **Leave Sanctioning:** routine leave sanctioned at the RO; maternity, sabbatical and study leave need higher approval. Leave calculation and salary processing demonstrated via a temporary access ID.
- **Salary & Allowance Processing:** RO's role in processing pay, permissible allowances, and TA/conveyance claims for official travel between branches.

---

## Slide 5 — Transfers, Discipline & RABD

- **Transfer Policy:** inter-state transfers involve management, unions and the Head Office; intra-region transfers are handled directly by the RO.
- **Disciplinary Procedure:** reporting of an issue, examination by the concerned authority, and a formal inquiry where necessary.
- **RABD & Priority Sector Lending:** agricultural lending, rural development schemes, and mandatory credit to agriculture, MSMEs, education, housing and weaker sections — the bank's role in financial inclusion.

---

## Slide 6 — Branch Visits & Data Collection

- **Fieldwork Scope:** visited 27 branches across the region plus RO departments (Retail Loans, Currency Chest) to administer a questionnaire on training effectiveness.
- **Staffing Observations:** branch strength varied widely — e.g. Triplicane 2 operated with just two officers and one cashier, directly limiting time available for training.
- **Training Perceptions:** employees compared classroom, online and live-streamed training, and how far they could apply what they learned at work.

---

## Slide 7 — Post-Amalgamation Change Management

- **The Merger:** Andhra Bank and Corporation Bank amalgamated with Union Bank of India, bringing employees from different legacy systems together.
- **SOP Adoption:** staff had to learn common Standard Operating Procedures for documentation, authorisation levels and customer service.
- **Training as Change Management:** continuous training, not one-time orientation, helped employees adapt with confidence to the merged organisation's systems.

---

## Slide 8 — Key Learnings

- **Broad HR Perspective:** HR in a public-sector bank goes beyond recruitment and salary — it covers welfare, leave, transfers, training, internal communication and industrial relations.
- **Core Practical Takeaways:**
  - Confidentiality and controlled access are central to handling employee records.
  - Staffing levels and branch workload directly shape training participation.
  - Hybrid learning (classroom + digital + live) works best when matched to the branch's operating conditions.
  - Training is a key tool of change management during organisational transitions.
- **Research Skills:** developed professional communication, research ethics and respectful data collection in a live banking environment.

---

## Slide 9 — Conclusion

- **Theory to Practice:** connected classroom learning at MSSW with practical HR administration and field research at a public-sector bank.
- **Operational Grounding:** gained exposure to HR functions, branch-level operations, RABD, currency chest and security functions.
- **Professional Growth:** improved understanding of confidentiality, professional conduct, and how HR supports the smooth functioning of a large organisation.
- **Dissertation Value:** the fieldwork directly informed the dissertation on Hybrid Learning and Training Effectiveness.
