## B. Organisation & Structure

17. **Explain the bank's administrative structure.** Head/Central Office (Mumbai) → Zonal Office → Regional Office → Branches.
18. **Where is the Head Office?** Mumbai.
19. **What is a Zonal Office?** An intermediate controlling office above Regional Offices that oversees multiple regions.
20. **What does the Regional Office do?** Administers ~75 branches: welfare, attendance, leave, intra-region transfers, salary/allowance processing, TA bills and routine IR.
21. **How many branches under Chennai South?** Approximately 75.
22. **Which functions are centralised at Head Office?** Recruitment, salary structure, benefits, disciplinary and IR policy, and broad L&D policy.
23. **Which recruitment is done centrally?** Clerical, probationary officer and specialist posts (Agriculture, IT, Marketing, HR, Legal, Hindi Officer).
24. **Why is policy centralised?** Consistency across the whole organisation.
25. **How does the RO "translate" policy?** By processing pay, allowances and claims and administering staff within the region according to Head Office rules.
26. **Which departments did you get exposure to?** HR desk, RABD, Retail Loans, Currency Chest, plus the branch network.
27. **What is the difference between a branch and a regional office?** Branches serve customers; the RO administers branches and staff.
28. **What type of bank is Union Bank of India?** A public-sector bank.
29. **What are the social responsibilities of a PSU bank?** Beyond profit: financial inclusion, priority-sector lending and service to underserved sections.

## F. Hybrid Learning & Training Effectiveness

88. **What is hybrid learning?** A combination of classroom training, online modules, live virtual sessions and work-based application.
89. **What are the three modes you studied?** Classroom, online modules, live-streamed sessions.
90. **What are the advantages of online/live-streamed learning?** Flexibility, less travel, easier scheduling.
91. **What are the advantages of classroom learning?** Direct interaction, discussion, demonstration and immediate clarification.
92. **What are the drawbacks of each?** Digital: less interaction. Classroom: time and travel constraints.
93. **Which mode did employees prefer?** State from your data; the field view was that a balanced mix suits banking.
94. **Is hybrid always better?** No; it depends on the training objective, workload and post-training support.
95. **How do you define training effectiveness?** Not just attendance or completion; employees understand, retain and apply the learning at work.
96. **How would you measure effectiveness?** Perceived usefulness, ability to apply on the job, confidence, and consistency of work practice.
97. **Why does workload matter?** In busy branches staff must prioritise customers and transactions, leaving little time for training.
98. **How did staffing affect training?** E.g. Triplicane 2 ran with two officers and one cashier, limiting time available.
99. **Why is digital learning useful for busy branches?** Employees can learn without leaving the branch for long.
100. **What factors influence effectiveness?** Relevance of content, access and timing, interaction and clarification, managerial support, follow-up and application.
101. **What is "relevance of content"?** Training is more meaningful when it links to the employee's role.
102. **What role does the manager play?** Provides time, platform access and encouragement to complete and apply learning.
103. **Why is follow-up important?** Reinforcement helps convert learning into regular work practice.
104. **What did the manager at Alandur tell you?** That employees must keep updating themselves, and that training also covers customer service and communication.
105. **Does training only cover technical skills?** No; also soft skills such as customer service and communication.
106. **What did you recommend?** Blended approach by objective, planning around branch conditions, post-digital clarification, post-merger support, and work-based reinforcement. These are broad, non-binding suggestions.
107. **Are your suggestions binding on the bank?** No; training decisions follow the bank's policies and L&D systems.
108. **What is a job aid?** A short reference tool (checklist, quick guide) that supports on-the-job application.
109. **Why short live Q&A after online modules?** It lets employees clarify doubts and apply learning with more confidence.
110. **Why is training "not an isolated HR activity"?** It links to technology, accuracy, compliance, customer service and change.

## H. Training & HR Theory (likely examiner questions)

122. **Training vs development?** Training builds job-specific skills now; development prepares for future roles.
123. **What is a Training Needs Analysis?** Identifying gaps between required and current skills at organisation, task and individual levels.
124. **What is the Kirkpatrick model?** Four levels: Reaction, Learning, Behaviour, Results.
125. **Where does your study sit on Kirkpatrick?** Mainly Reaction and perceived Learning/Behaviour (application at work), based on perceptions.
126. **What is ADDIE?** Analysis, Design, Development, Implementation, Evaluation.
127. **What is blended learning vs hybrid learning?** Often used interchangeably; blended mixes online and face-to-face, hybrid also covers flexible live/virtual delivery. State the definition from your literature review.
128. **What is transfer of training?** Applying learned skills on the job.
129. **What affects transfer of training?** Supervisor support, opportunity to apply, relevance and reinforcement.
130. **What is adult learning (andragogy)?** Adults learn best when content is relevant, practical and self-directed (Knowles).
131. **What is on-the-job vs off-the-job training?** On the job at the workplace; off the job in classrooms or outside.
132. **What is e-learning, m-learning, synchronous and asynchronous learning?** E-learning is electronic; m-learning is mobile; synchronous is live; asynchronous is self-paced.
133. **What is a learning management system (LMS)?** A platform for delivering and tracking digital learning.
134. **Why do public-sector banks have mandatory training?** Regulation, compliance, technology change and consistency of service.
135. **What is job satisfaction vs engagement?** Satisfaction is contentment; engagement is energy and commitment toward work.
136. **How does training relate to retention and performance?** Capable, supported employees perform better and are more likely to stay (state evidence only from your review).
