**Research date:** 9 October 2026

## 30-second overview

Godrej Agrovet Limited (GAVL), part of the Godrej Industries Group, is a diversified Indian food and agribusiness. Its purpose is to innovate to improve farmer productivity and help feed the nation. Its current website presents Animal Nutrition, Oil Palm, Crop Care, Foods, and Astec LifeSciences as business areas. The business operates across the agricultural value chain, from farmer-facing products and advisory to manufacturing and food. [1][2][3]

## What the businesses do

- **Animal Nutrition:** Cattle, poultry, fish, shrimp, and other feed, with farmer support and R&D. The company's animal nutrition page describes its Indian operation as the largest private compound feed player. [4]
- **Crop Care:** Crop protection, plant growth regulators, and soil-health products; the company highlights farmer productivity and partnerships for product development. [5]
- **Oil Palm:** Cultivation partnerships and processing. In July 2026 the company announced an integrated oil palm complex in Telangana with a mill, R&D capabilities, and a farmer support centre. [6]
- **Foods and Astec LifeSciences:** Additional businesses shown in the company's portfolio. Read the relevant business page if the offered HR role is attached to one of them. [1]
