## Registration sheet — verify each field

- **Email:** A working **personal** email only, never the college email. Test communication goes to this address. Check the spelling and access to the inbox.
- **Address:** Permanent address, not college or hostel address.
- **Academic percentages:** Simple average percentages of **all subjects and all years/semesters** for Class 10, Class 12, graduation, and post-graduation, following the attached sample exactly. Do not substitute CGPA, selected subjects, or a weighted aggregate unless the sample explicitly instructs it. Ask the placement cell how to handle a current, incomplete programme.
- **Practice preferences:** Up to **two HRD practices**, in order of preference. Infosys considers preferences, but makes the final shortlist based on profile at its discretion. The available practice names must come from the actual sheet/mail.
- **Previous Infosys association:** Declare if the student is an ex-Infoscion or on sabbatical from Infosys, and include the Infosys employee number where applicable. Otherwise follow the sheet's prescribed blank/No format.
- **Work experience:** For prior work experience to be considered, Infosys requires all three: salary credited to a bank account **every month**, **Form 16** for the period, and a **PF account**. Do not present an internship as qualifying work experience merely because it appears on the resume. Clarify any ambiguous field with the placement cell.
- **Consistency:** Names, dates, education, percentages, and employment claims should match supporting documents. The message warns that later background-verification discrepancies may affect CTC.
