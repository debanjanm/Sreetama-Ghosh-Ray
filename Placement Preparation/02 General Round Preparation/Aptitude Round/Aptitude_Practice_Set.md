# Aptitude Round — Solved Practice and Timed Set

These are illustrative questions, not a prediction of any company's test. Check the actual test pattern, timing, calculator rules, and negative marking before practising for a specific company.

## Worked examples

1. **Percentages:** A training programme has 80 participants; attendance rises by 25%. How many attend now? **Solution:** 80 × 1.25 = **100**.
2. **Successive change:** A fee of ₹1,000 rises 20% and then falls 10%. **Solution:** 1,000 × 1.20 × 0.90 = **₹1,080**, an 8% net increase.
3. **Ratio:** Officers and clerks are in a 3:5 ratio among 64 respondents. How many clerks? **Solution:** 64 × 5/8 = **40**.
4. **Average:** Five scores average 72. A sixth score is 90. New average? **Solution:** (5 × 72 + 90)/6 = **75**.
5. **Time and work:** A finishes a task in 6 days and B in 12. Together? **Solution:** 1/6 + 1/12 = 1/4 task per day, so **4 days**.
6. **Data interpretation:** Applications by channel: referral 40, campus 60, portal 100. What share came from campus? **Solution:** Total 200; 60/200 = **30%**.
7. **Probability:** A bag has 3 red and 2 blue balls. One ball is selected. Probability of blue? **Solution:** **2/5**.
8. **Syllogism:** All HR interns are students. Some students are researchers. Does “some HR interns are researchers” necessarily follow? **Solution:** **No**; the two subsets may not overlap.
9. **Verbal:** “Neither the manager nor the employees ___ available.” **Answer:** **are**; the nearer plural subject controls agreement in this construction.

## Timed set — 12 questions, 15 minutes

Attempt before looking at the key. Record time taken and the reason for each error.

1. A value rises from 240 to 300. What is the percentage increase?
2. An item priced ₹800 has successive discounts of 10% and 5%. What is the final price?
3. A team has men:women = 2:3 and 45 members. How many women?
4. Four values average 18. Three are 12, 20, and 24. Find the fourth.
5. A completes a task in 8 days and B in 24 days. How long together?
6. A survey received 120 responses: 54 online and 66 paper. What percentage was online?
7. A fair die is rolled once. What is the probability of an even number?
8. Find the next number: 3, 7, 15, 31, __.
9. A person walks 4 km north, then 3 km east. What is the straight-line distance from the start?
10. All trainers are employees. No employee is a contractor. Can a trainer be a contractor under these premises?
11. Choose the correct sentence: (A) Each of the candidates have applied. (B) Each of the candidates has applied.
12. A passage says a pilot improved attendance but gives no evidence about learning outcomes. Which conclusion is justified? (A) Learning improved. (B) Attendance improved. (C) The pilot failed.

## Answer key and reasoning

1. **25%:** (300 − 240)/240 × 100.
2. **₹684:** 800 × .90 × .95.
3. **27:** 45 × 3/5.
4. **16:** total 72 minus 12 + 20 + 24 = 56.
5. **6 days:** 1/8 + 1/24 = 1/6 per day.
6. **45%:** 54/120 × 100.
7. **1/2:** three even faces out of six.
8. **63:** each term doubles and adds 1.
9. **5 km:** right triangle, √(4² + 3²).
10. **No:** every trainer is an employee, and no employee is a contractor.
11. **B:** “Each” takes a singular verb.
12. **B:** the passage supports attendance only.
