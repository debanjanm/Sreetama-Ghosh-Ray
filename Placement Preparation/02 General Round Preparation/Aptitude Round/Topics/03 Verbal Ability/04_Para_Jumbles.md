# Para Jumbles

## Method
Find a sentence that introduces the subject without unresolved “this/it/they.” Pair references with antecedents, then use chronology and cause→effect. Read the complete sequence aloud.

## Worked pattern
A. This feedback led to a revised schedule.  
B. New joiners said the first week was overloaded.  
C. The revised schedule spread sessions across two weeks.  
D. HR asked new joiners about their induction.

**Order: D→B→A→C.** D introduces the survey; B is feedback; “This feedback” in A points to B; C gives the resulting revision.

## Tips and traps
An opening can sometimes begin with a pronoun if it refers to a general concept, so do not use a mechanical ban. Look for definite articles (“the policy”) versus first introductions (“a policy”), contrast words, and paired examples.

## Try it
P. It was then reviewed by the manager. Q. An intern prepared a draft. R. The approved version was sent. **Answer:** Q→P→R.
