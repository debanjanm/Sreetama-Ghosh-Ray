# Percentages

## Rules and formulas
- Percentage change = (new−old)/old ×100.
- New value after p% rise/fall = old ×(1±p/100).
- Successive signed changes a%, b% combine to a+b+ab/100 percent.
- To reverse a change, divide by the multiplier; never simply add back the stated percentage.

## Recognise the pattern
“What percent of” → part/whole. “Increase from” → old is denominator. “After a discount/increase, find original” → divide by remaining/growth fraction.

## Solve
1. A value rises from 80 to 100. Rise =20/80=**25%**. The fall from 100 back to 80 is 20/100=**20%**: bases differ.
2. After a 20% reduction, an amount is ₹640. Original =640/0.8=**₹800**.
3. Rise 20%, fall 10%: 1.2×0.9=1.08, an **8% net rise**.

## Tips and traps
Memorise 1/8=12.5%, 1/6≈16.67%, 1/5=20%, 1/4=25%, 1/3≈33.33%. Approximate only if answer choices are sufficiently apart.

## Try it
After a 25% increase a price is ₹500. Original? **Answer:** ₹400.
