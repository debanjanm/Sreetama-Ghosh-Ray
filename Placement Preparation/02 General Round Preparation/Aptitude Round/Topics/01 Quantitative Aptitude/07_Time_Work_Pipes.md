# Time and Work; Pipes

## Rules and formulas
- If A takes a days, rate is 1/a task/day.
- Combined rate is the sum of rates; subtract an outlet/leak rate.
- Time = work remaining / net rate.

## Recognise the pattern
“Together,” “one leaves,” “pipe fills/empties,” “twice as efficient” → rates, not times. Efficiency and completion time are inverse.

## Solve
1. A takes 6 days, B 12: net 1/6+1/12=1/4; **4 days**.
2. Together for two days complete 1/2. A alone completes remaining 1/2 in (1/2)/(1/6)=**3 days**.
3. Inlet fills in 4 h, outlet empties in 12 h: rate 1/4−1/12=1/6; **6 h**.

## Tips and traps
Take LCM of given days as total units to avoid fractions. If net rate is zero or negative, the tank will not fill from empty. Clearly distinguish “A leaves after 2 days” from “B leaves.”

## Try it
A takes 10 days, B 15. Together? **Answer:** 1/(1/10+1/15)=6 days.
