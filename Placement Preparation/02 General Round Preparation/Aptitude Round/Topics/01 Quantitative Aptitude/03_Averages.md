# Averages

## Rules and formulas
- Average = total/count; total = average×count.
- Combined average = (n₁a₁+n₂a₂)/(n₁+n₂).
- Replacing one value changes average by (new−old)/count.

## Recognise the pattern
“One person joins/leaves,” “two groups combined,” or “one score was recorded incorrectly” → work with totals first.

## Solve
1. Ten people average 60, twenty average 75. Total =600+1500=2100, count=30; combined average **70**.
2. Six scores average 40. One recorded as 30 should be 48. Correct total =240−30+48=258; correct average **43**.
3. Five consecutive integers average 18. The middle integer is 18; sequence **16,17,18,19,20**.

## Tips and traps
The combined average lies between group averages. A plain mean of two averages is valid only if group sizes match. For “average increases by x after one joins,” new value = old average + (new count)×x.

## Try it
Eight values average 12; a ninth value of 21 joins. New average? **Answer:** (96+21)/9=13.
