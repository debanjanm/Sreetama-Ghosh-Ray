# Experience Round — Resume Defence and Story Bank

Use the latest resume alongside this file. Questions below are prompts, not evidence that an activity happened exactly as described. For every answer, separate **observed**, **assisted**, and **personally executed** work. Confirm numbers and dates against records before interviews; never disclose confidential employee or bank information.

## Universal answer pattern

**Context → my responsibility → steps I personally took → checks/constraints → outcome → learning.** For each bullet on the resume, prepare a 30-second answer and a two-minute deep dive. If asked for a result that was not measured, say so plainly.

## Union Bank of India — HR administration and fieldwork

1. What was the difference between the Zonal and Regional Office work you observed?
2. Which HR tasks did you perform yourself, and which did you only observe (leave, salary, transfer, discipline)?
3. How did you approach 27 branches without disrupting customer service? What permissions or coordination were needed?
4. How did you distribute, collect, and check questionnaires? What did you do with incomplete responses?
5. What employee information did you avoid collecting, and how did you protect the survey data?
6. What did you learn about post-amalgamation retraining and adoption of standard procedures? Distinguish staff views from documented findings.
7. Describe one logistical setback during fieldwork and the real action you took.

**Evidence to have ready:** branch visit log, survey instrument, response counts, eligibility rules, and a brief explanation of her own role. Do not bring identifiable respondent data to an interview.

## GPT Group — onboarding and HR operations

1. Walk through a new joiner’s process from offer to completed file. Where did you enter the workflow?
2. How did you verify offer, appointment, and confirmation letters? Which fields were checked?
3. Which documents belonged in the onboarding file, and how did you handle missing or inconsistent information?
4. What fields did you maintain in the Employee Master Sheet? How did you avoid errors and restrict access?
5. The resume says **100% onboarding adherence**. What was the denominator, time period, and evidence? If this cannot be verified, revise the claim before an interview.
6. What checks did you make when preparing ID cards? What did you do if information conflicted?
7. What did you learn about secure archiving of resigned employee records?

**Story candidate:** a real documentation discrepancy, how she checked it, whom she informed, and the verified resolution.

## IOCL — payroll, employee relations, and L&D

1. Explain the pro-rata salary calculation for a hypothetical employee with leave without pay. What assumptions and checks did you use?
2. What exactly did you do for the 30 employees: independent calculation, supervised exercise, or production processing?
3. How did you compare the labour law compendium with newer material? What did you flag for review rather than decide yourself?
4. What was the purpose of the chargesheet exercise? Was it a draft or an issued document? What review was required?
5. How were RTI requests handled by the payroll team, and what part did you personally support?
6. What was the purpose and sample of the 25+ employee digital-systems survey? What could and could not be concluded?
7. What did you contribute to officer induction and TNA questionnaire refinement?

**Story candidate:** learning a new HR process, checking calculations or documents with a supervisor, and improving accuracy.

## Dissertation — hybrid learning and training effectiveness

1. What was the research question, and why use a cross-sectional employee survey?
2. Define Hybrid Learning, Trainer Competence, Learner Engagement, and Training Effectiveness in plain language.
3. How did you pilot the 30-question instrument with 20 employees? What changed after the pilot?
4. Explain **132 collected → 103 eligible → 102 valid regression cases**. What rule removed each group of records?
5. How were the 27 branches selected? What sampling limitations follow?
6. What does **R² = .61** mean? Does it prove hybrid learning caused effectiveness? **No:** it describes variation explained by predictors in this model, not causation.
7. Cronbach’s alpha is reported as **.98**. For which scale(s) was this computed? Was item redundancy examined? Confirm the exact analysis before citing it.
8. What did regression add beyond correlation, and what assumptions or diagnostics did you check?
9. What are the study’s main limitations and one practical recommendation for bank training?
10. What work did she perform personally versus what a guide or others helped with?

## Resume checks before applying

- Resolve the degree-name inconsistency between the resume summary and education section.
- Confirm internship end dates and whether an internship still in progress is described in past tense.
- Verify all numbers (27 branches, 30 employees, 25+ surveyed, 132/103/102 responses, 61%, 100% adherence) and define what each counts.
- Check that labour-law statements reflect what she studied and what was in force at the relevant time; avoid presenting a classroom comparison as a legal compliance audit unless that was the actual assignment.
